Turkey Baked Ziti

Freeform lasagna: ziti, turkey-tomato sauce, ricotta dollops, mozzarella. Hits the cooked-tomato quota without cloning Spaghetti & Meatballs or Turkey Meatballs in Tomato Sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1.5 lb ground turkey
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 3 oz tomato paste (half a 6 oz can — freeze the rest)
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 Tbsp Italian seasoning (doubled — turkey needs more seasoning than sausage)
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 tsp kosher salt, plus 1/2 Tbsp for the pasta water
  • Black pepper
  • 1 lb ziti, penne, or rigatoni
  • 1 cup ricotta
  • 1.5 cups shredded mozzarella
  • Optional: handful chopped parsley

Method

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium. Add the turkey and cook, breaking it up, until browned with no pink remaining. Add the onion and cook until soft, about 5 minutes.
  2. Stir in tomato paste, crushed tomatoes, Italian seasoning, water, salt, and pepper. Lid on, simmer while the pasta cooks. Stir occasionally.
  3. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the ziti 1–2 minutes short of the box time (it finishes in the oven). Drain.
  4. Heat oven to 350°F. Return the pasta to the pot and stir in about 1 cup of the sauce so it isn't dry.
  5. In a 9×13 dish: half the pasta, dollops of half the ricotta, half the remaining sauce. Repeat. Top with mozzarella.
  6. Cover loosely with foil (tent so it doesn't stick to the cheese). Bake 20 minutes. Uncover and broil 3–5 minutes until the top is browned — watch it. Parsley if you have it.

Notes

  • Budget Bytes writes this with Italian sausage and a full 15 oz ricotta + Italian-blend + 2 cups mozzarella. House version: turkey (Sam's/Aldi), olive oil to brown it, Italian seasoning doubled (Budget Bytes' own swap note), ricotta cut to 1 cup so it stays moderate not a cheese casserole.
  • Make-ahead: assemble, cool, foil, fridge up to 24 hours. Add ~10 minutes covered bake from cold.
  • Pasta is the starch — no rice.
  • Mediterranean ✓: olive oil, poultry, tomato/onion, not red meat. Regular pasta is refined so it misses "whole-ish carbs," but 3 of 4 still clears.
  • Not Lentil Bolognese (no lentils, baked not stovetop sauce) and not the meatball nights.
