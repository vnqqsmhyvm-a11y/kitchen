Corn Flake Chicken

Ingredients

  • 6 boneless skinless chicken breast halves
  • 3 cups crushed corn flakes (~6 cups whole)
  • 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1-1/2 tsp seasoned salt
  • 3 large eggs, beaten (source uses 1 cup egg substitute)
  • 1/4 cup butter, melted

Method

  1. Preheat 350°. Coat a 13x9 baking dish with some of the melted butter.
  2. Mix corn flakes, flour, seasoned salt in a shallow dish; beaten eggs in another.
  3. Dip chicken in egg, press into corn flake mixture to coat. Place in dish; drizzle remaining butter over top.
  4. Bake uncovered 20 min; flip; bake 10-15 min more to 170-175° internal.
