Shepherd's Pie Casserole

Classic shepherd's / cottage pie: ground beef with onions, carrots, peas, tomato paste, and Worcestershire under mashed potatoes and cheddar. Not Vegetarian Lentil Shepherd's Pie (lentil gravy, no meat, no cheddar top).

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef (or ground lamb)
  • 1 cup diced onions
  • 1 cup diced carrots
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 4 cups mashed potatoes
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese

Method

Source method (Amanda Flavours thread) — same bake for all six; only the mix and a couple of assembly notes change.

  1. Prep: Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Grease a 9×13-inch dish. Brown the ground beef with the onions, carrots, and garlic; drain. Stir in the tomato paste, Worcestershire, and peas; cook 1–2 minutes. Make or warm the mashed potatoes and set aside.
  2. Mix / Assemble: Spread the meat mixture in the bottom of the dish. Top with the mashed potatoes, then the cheddar.
  3. Bake uncovered 25–30 minutes until the cheese is melted and the edges bubble. Cover loosely with foil if the top browns too fast.
  4. Rest 5 minutes before serving so it sets.

Notes

  • Servings: source says serves 8 / bake 30 min. Household is 6 — keep the original amounts; leftovers cover lunches.
  • Red meat: ground beef (or lamb) as the main counts toward the ≤2 red/processed-meat cap. No chicken swap needed.
  • a fish-free plate / hidden fish: this is not a fish dish, but Worcestershire often contains anchovy. Use a fish-free Worcestershire (or skip it) for the fish-allergic eater / the household. Check the label.
  • WW tier moderate: red meat with a full vegetable base and potato top; 1 cup cheddar is not cheese-heavy. Not fried or cream-soup based. Classed like the house beef standards (Chili), not like Meatlovers Pizza Casserole.
  • Mediterranean ✗: red meat ✗; vegetables ✓ and potatoes ✓; fat is not olive-oil-forward (2 of 4).
  • Cooked tomato: tomato paste is a small cooked-tomato boost; do not rely on it alone for the weekly tomato target.
  • Distinct from Vegetarian Lentil Shepherd's Pie — keep both cards; do not merge.
  • Nutrition: not given by the source; none listed here.
