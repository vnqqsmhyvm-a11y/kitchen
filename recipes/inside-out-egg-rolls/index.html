Inside-Out Egg Rolls

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground sausage (house tweak: ground turkey keeps it light-tier)
  • 16 oz shredded cabbage (or 2 bags coleslaw mix)
  • 2 tsp minced garlic
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 Tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup chopped green onion, for garnish

Method

  1. Brown the meat in a large skillet, crumbling as it cooks.
  2. Add cabbage, garlic, ginger, soy sauce; cook until the cabbage softens but isn't mushy.
  3. Off heat, top with green onions. Serve immediately.

Notes

  • Source: https://myproductivebackyard.com/low-carb-egg-roll-in-a-bowl/ (20 min, one skillet).
  • Usually a hit — keep a plain protein on hand for picky eaters.
  • Cabbage = the cruciferous flag; one of the few dishes that hits it.
