Sheet-Pan Chickpeas, Feta & Tomatoes

Ali Slagle's NYT sheet-pan: chickpeas, cherry tomatoes, honey, chile, and slabs of feta, roasted until the cheese goes soft. No chicken. Serve over brown rice.

Ingredients

  • 3 cans (15 oz) chickpeas, drained, rinsed, and shaken dry
  • 2 pints cherry tomatoes
  • 1 small yellow onion or 1 shallot, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp honey
  • 1/2 tsp red-pepper flakes (pinch only — or skip — for kids)
  • Kosher salt
  • 2 blocks feta (6–8 oz each), sliced 1-inch thick
  • Brown rice, cooked, to serve (house rule: always brown)
  • Optional: handful of spinach or arugula scattered on after roasting; lemon wedges

Method

  1. Heat oven to 400°F.
  2. On a sheet pan, stir together the chickpeas, tomatoes, onion, olive oil, honey, and red-pepper flakes. Season with salt. Spread in an even layer.
  3. Nestle the feta slabs among the chickpeas.
  4. Roast 30–35 minutes, no stirring, until the tomatoes burst, the chickpeas are golden, and the feta is soft.
  5. Eat right away over brown rice. The feta hardens as it cools — reheat leftovers.

Notes

  • Scaled from NYT (2 cans chickpeas / serves 4) to 3 cans for 6. Onion stands in for shallot (Aldi/Sam's). Skip gochugaru — red-pepper flakes are already in the pantry; go light for kids.
  • NYT tip: avoid cow's-milk feta, which can dry out. Sheep/goat feta (the block in brine) melts better. Grocery-store block is fine on probation.
  • moderate tier: two blocks of feta + honey. Still vegetable-and-legume forward, not a cheese casserole.
  • Mediterranean ✓: olive oil, legumes, tomatoes, brown rice. No chicken — that's the point (Greek chicken bowls and lemon-herb sheet chicken already cover that).
