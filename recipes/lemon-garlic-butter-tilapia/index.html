Lemon Garlic Butter Tilapia

Pan-seared tilapia in a silky lemon-butter-garlic sauce — bright, mild, ready in about 20 minutes. A tilapia-swap-week option.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb tilapia fillets (about 6)
  • 1/4 cup flour, for dredging (optional, for a light crust)
  • Salt and pepper
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 3 Tbsp butter
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/3 cup chicken broth
  • Juice and zest of 1 lemon
  • 2 Tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

Method

  1. Pat the tilapia dry and season with salt and pepper (dredge lightly in flour if using).
  2. Heat the olive oil and 1 Tbsp of the butter in a skillet over medium-high. Cook the fillets 3–4 minutes per side until golden and flaky. Remove and keep warm.
  3. Lower the heat, add the remaining butter and the garlic, and cook 30 seconds until fragrant. Add the broth, lemon juice, and zest; simmer 2–3 minutes until slightly thickened.
  4. Spoon the sauce over the fish and sprinkle with parsley. Serve with rice or vegetables.

Notes

  • Fish-free swap: quesadillas, or a plain lemon-butter chicken breast from the bag.
  • Lean white fish — no omega-3. Does NOT count toward the fatty-fish quota (tilapia-swap week per Diet Lens).
  • mediterranean: false — butter (not olive oil) is the finishing fat.
