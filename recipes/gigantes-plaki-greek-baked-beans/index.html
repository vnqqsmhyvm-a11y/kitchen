Gigantes Plaki (Greek Baked Giant Beans)

The Mediterranean Dish's gigantes plaki: big butter beans baked in a thick tomato sauce built on olive oil, onion, carrot, celery, and garlic, scented with oregano, thyme, parsley, bay, and a whisper of cinnamon, finished with olive oil and crumbled feta. Greek taverna food; mop it up with crusty bread.

Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup extra-virgin olive oil, plus more to finish
  • 1 1/2 large onions, roughly chopped
  • 3 large carrots, peeled and chopped
  • 3 celery stalks, chopped
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  • ~1 1/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 3 cans (15 oz) diced tomatoes, with juices
  • 1 cup + 2 Tbsp water
  • 3/4 cup chopped fresh parsley, plus more to garnish
  • 1 1/2 tsp dried thyme (or 1 1/2 Tbsp fresh)
  • 2 1/4 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes (optional; source uses 3/4–1 1/2 tsp at this scale)
  • 3/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 3 cans (15 oz) butter beans (large lima), drained and rinsed
  • 4–6 oz feta, crumbled
  • To serve: crusty whole-grain bread; brown rice for anyone who wants it

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F.
  2. In a large oven-safe skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high, heat the olive oil. Add the onion, carrot, celery, and garlic with 1/2 tsp salt; cook 5–7 minutes until soft.
  3. Add the tomatoes and water, another 1/2 tsp salt, and the parsley, thyme, oregano, red pepper, black pepper, cinnamon, and bay leaves.
  4. Stir in the beans; cook 5 minutes over high heat. Taste and adjust. (If your skillet is small, pour everything into a 9x13 dish now.)
  5. Bake uncovered 25–30 minutes until thick and lightly golden on top.
  6. Remove the bay leaves; finish with a drizzle of olive oil, parsley, and feta. Serve with bread.

Notes

  • Tripled from The Mediterranean Dish's 1 can / 4 servings (side-dish portions) to 3 cans so it holds up as a meatless main for 6.
  • Butter beans stand in for true Greek gigantes, as Suzy recommends ("or the largest beans you can find") — Bush's/store-brand butter beans at Sam's or Aldi.
  • Kid heat: red pepper flakes cut to a pinch; cinnamon and oregano carry the flavor.
  • Not Sheet-Pan Chickpeas with Feta and Tomatoes — that's roasted chickpeas, cherry tomatoes, honey, and slabs of feta; this is a braised tomato-vegetable sauce with butter beans and herbs, feta only as garnish. Close cousins; don't schedule both in one week.
  • Mediterranean ✓ (4 of 4 with whole-grain bread). light.
