Brazilian Fish Stew (Moqueca)

Allergy: Contains fish or shellfish — not for a fish-allergic eater; same-broth chicken stew is in this card

RecipeTin Eats' moqueca baiana: lime-marinated white fish seared golden, then simmered briefly in a coconut-tomato broth with peppers, onion, cumin, and paprika, finished with lime and cilantro. Latin American fish night — bright, a little sweet, not spicy without the cayenne.

Ingredients

Fish

  • 1.5 lb firm white fish (cod, pollock, haddock), skinless, 1-inch cubes
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp lime juice
  • 1/3 tsp salt, black pepper
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp olive oil (marinade) + 1 1/2 Tbsp for searing

Broth

  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 1/2 small onions, finely diced
  • 1 1/2 large red bell peppers, halved and sliced
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp ground cumin
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp paprika
  • Cayenne: skip for kids (source uses 1 tsp for 4, optional)
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • 1 1/2 cans (14 oz) coconut milk
  • 1 1/2 cans (14 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 1/2 cups chicken or vegetable broth (not fish stock — see Notes)
  • 2–3 Tbsp lime juice, plus wedges
  • 1/4 cup chopped cilantro, plus more to serve
  • Brown rice, cooked, to serve (house rule: always brown)

Fish-free fallback (same broth)

  • 1 boneless chicken breast or 2 thighs, 1-inch cubes

Method

  1. Toss the fish with lime juice, oil, salt, and pepper; refrigerate 20 minutes.
  2. Broth in a Dutch oven (not the fish skillet): heat 2 Tbsp oil over medium-high; cook garlic and onion 1 1/2 minutes. Add peppers; 2 minutes. Add sugar, cumin, paprika, salt, coconut milk, tomatoes, and broth. Simmer on medium 15–20 minutes until thickened.
  3. Meanwhile, in a large skillet over high heat, sear the fish-free chicken first in a little oil until cooked through (165°F). Set aside on its own plate.
  4. Wipe the skillet, add 1 1/2 Tbsp oil, and sear the fish until just cooked and lightly golden. Set aside.
  5. Ladle the fish-free broth (about 1 1/2 cups) into a small saucepan with the fish-free chicken; warm through.
  6. Return the fish to the big pot for ~2 minutes. Stir in lime juice and cilantro.
  7. Serve over brown rice with lime wedges.

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.5 from RecipeTin's serves 4–5 (1 lb fish).
  • Fish-free method tweak: Nagi makes the broth in the same skillet the fish was seared in. This card splits it — broth in its own pot, chicken seared before fish — so the fish-free portion never touches fish.
  • Broth: Nagi lists fish stock or chicken/vegetable; use chicken or vegetable.
  • Lean white fish — no omega-3. Doesn't count toward the fatty-fish quota.
  • Mediterranean ✓ on 3 of 4: olive oil, fish, peppers + tomato, brown rice (coconut milk keeps it moderate).
