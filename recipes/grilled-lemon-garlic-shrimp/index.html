Grilled Lemon Garlic Shrimp

Allergy: Contains fish or shellfish — not for a fish-allergic eater; same-sauce chicken is in this card

Kenji's Food Lab grilled shrimp: salt + baking soda brine, snug double-skewers, garlic-lemon oil. Grill, not steak/chops/ribs. Kid-mild — no cayenne.

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 lb large shrimp (15–20 per pound), peeled and deveined
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1 Tbsp kosher salt (use half if table salt)
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced, divided
  • 1/4 cup olive oil, divided
  • Zest of 1 lemon + 4 tsp lemon juice
  • 2 Tbsp chopped parsley (optional)
  • Brown rice, cooked, to serve (house rule: always brown)
  • Vegetable side: zucchini, bell pepper, or a bag of salad

Fish-free fallback (same sauce, same grill)

  • 2 boneless chicken breasts (~1 lb)
  • Extra of the garlic-olive oil + lemon (don't share the shrimp bowl)

Method

  1. Toss shrimp with baking soda, salt, and sugar. Thread onto sets of 2 skewers, nestled snugly (about 6 shrimp per set). Rest in the fridge 15–60 minutes (1 hour is the source; 15 minutes still helps).
  2. Mix half the garlic with half the olive oil — this is the brushing oil. In a large bowl (shrimp only), mix remaining garlic, remaining olive oil, lemon zest, lemon juice, and parsley.
  3. Toss the fish-free chicken in a separate dish with a spoonful of garlic-olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  4. Heat the grill to high. Brush shrimp with the garlic oil. Grill shrimp over the hot side, turning, until just cooked and charred, 4–5 minutes total. Grill the chicken alongside until 165°F, ~6–8 minutes per side depending on thickness.
  5. Slide shrimp off skewers into the lemon-garlic bowl and toss. Slice the fish-free chicken. Serve over brown rice with the vegetable.

Notes

  • Baking soda is a pantry staple — it keeps the shrimp plump. Don't skip it.
  • Wooden skewers: soak 30 minutes. Metal is easier.
  • Fish-free swap: chicken breast, same garlic-lemon oil, same grill. Never toss the fish-free chicken in the shrimp bowl. Quesadillas only if you're not already at the grill.
  • Shrimp is shellfish, not fatty fish — does not count toward the salmon/omega-3 quota. Still a fish-night for the fish-free protocol.
  • Mediterranean ✓: olive oil, seafood over red meat, vegetable on the plate, brown rice.
