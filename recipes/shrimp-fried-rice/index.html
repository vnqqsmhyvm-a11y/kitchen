Shrimp Fried Rice

RecipeTin Eats' Chinese restaurant-style fried rice: scrambled egg, a little bacon, garlic, ginger, onion, and small shrimp tossed with day-old rice in an oyster-free soy sauce mix. Twenty minutes start to finish, and the best use of leftover brown rice in the rotation.

Ingredients

Sauce

  • 2 Tbsp hoisin sauce (replaces Nagi's oyster sauce — see Notes)
  • 3 Tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil

Fried rice

  • 6 Tbsp vegetable oil, divided
  • 4 eggs, lightly beaten
  • 4 slices bacon, chopped (optional — see Notes)
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 Tbsp minced ginger (jar ginger is fine)
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 Tbsp dry sherry or Chinese cooking wine (or chicken broth)
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 6 cups cooked brown rice, refrigerated overnight
  • 1 cup frozen peas and carrots (optional add)
  • 6 scallions, thinly sliced
  • 10 oz small cooked, peeled shrimp (thawed; frozen bag from Sam's or Aldi)

Fish-free fallback (same rice, same sauce)

  • 1 cup diced cooked chicken (leftover or rotisserie)

Method

  1. Stir the hoisin, soy sauce, and sesame oil together.
  2. Heat 1 Tbsp oil in a wok or your largest skillet over medium. Scramble the eggs, then move them to a plate.
  3. Raise heat to high; cook the bacon until golden, then add to the egg plate. Pour off most of the fat.
  4. Add the remaining oil, garlic, and ginger; when the garlic sizzles add the onion and stir-fry 2 minutes until golden.
  5. Add the sherry and sugar; let it bubble 20 seconds until mostly gone.
  6. Add the rice, peas and carrots, sauce, eggs, bacon, and most of the scallions. Stir-fry 3–4 minutes until the rice is hot (brown rice needs a minute or two longer than white).
  7. Pull the fish-free portion now: scoop about 1/6 of the fried rice into a separate pan or bowl and toss it with the chicken (microwave or warm it in a small skillet).
  8. Add the shrimp to the wok and stir-fry 1–2 minutes to heat through. Serve with the remaining scallions.

Notes

  • Scaled ×2 from RecipeTin Eats' 3 cups rice / serves 3.
  • Brown rice (house rule): day-old brown rice fries even better than white — drier grains. Cook a double batch earlier in the week.
  • Hidden-fish swap: Nagi's sauce uses oyster sauce (shellfish extract). Hoisin replaces it so the base rice is safe for the fish-allergic eater before the shrimp goes in.
  • Fish-free swap: shrimp goes in only after the fish-free portion is pulled; it's pre-cooked, so it just needs warming.
  • Bacon is processed meat — counts against the red/processed cap if used. Leave it out and add an extra egg to keep the night clean; still tastes like takeout.
  • Not Copycat Panda Teriyaki Chicken or Shrimp Stir Fry — this is a one-wok rice dish, not a stir-fry over rice.
  • Mediterranean ✗ (seafood + veg, but neutral oil and fried; 2 of 4). moderate.
