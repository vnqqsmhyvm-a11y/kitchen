Mediterranean Tuna Pasta

Allergy: Contains fish or shellfish — not for a fish-allergic eater; same-sauce chicken is in this card

Budget Bytes' tuna steaks poached right in a garlicky tomato-olive sauce with summer squash, served over pasta. Tomato night and fish night in one pan — the cooked-tomato quota and the fish quota both get a tick.

Ingredients

  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 1/2 yellow onions, diced
  • 2 1/4 tsp minced garlic
  • 4–5 anchovy fillets — optional, see Notes
  • 1 can (28 oz) + 1 can (15 oz) diced or crushed tomatoes
  • 3/4 cup kalamata olives, roughly chopped
  • 2 1/4 tsp dried basil
  • Pinch red pepper flakes (source: 1/4 tsp for 4 — cut for kids)
  • 3/4 tsp salt, black pepper
  • 3 medium zucchini/yellow squash, half-moons
  • 1.25 lb tuna steaks (frozen tuna steaks are fine, thawed)
  • 1/3 bunch parsley, chopped
  • 1 lb pasta (whole-wheat penne or spaghetti if you have it)

Fish-free fallback (same sauce, own pan)

  • 1 boneless chicken breast, halved into 2 thin cutlets

Method

  1. Cook onion and garlic (and anchovies, if using — see Notes) in the olive oil in a large deep skillet over medium until the onion is soft.
  2. Add the squash; sauté until slightly softened.
  3. Add the tomatoes, olives, basil, pepper flakes, salt, and pepper. Simmer.
  4. The fish-free pan: ladle ~1 1/2 cups sauce into a small skillet, nestle in the fish-free chicken cutlets, cover, and simmer ~12 minutes until 165°F.
  5. Nestle the tuna steaks into the big skillet, cover, and simmer until the tuna turns from mauve to white all the way through, ~8–10 minutes.
  6. Cook the pasta; drain. Lift out the tuna, stir parsley and pasta into the sauce, and serve topped with tuna (a fish-free plate: the fish-free chicken and sauce over plain pasta, served first with a clean spoon).

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.5 from Budget Bytes' 12 oz tuna / 1/2 lb pasta (serves 4–6); pasta bumped to a full pound for this family.
  • Anchovies: Beth melts them into the sauce base. If you use them, the fish-free sauce must be ladled off before the anchovies go in (cook the fish-free onion/garlic share separately) — or just skip them; olives already bring the salt.
  • Tuna steaks are lean-ish; they count as fish but not reliably as fatty fish. Swap 2 lb salmon fillets in the same sauce for a fatty-fish week.
  • Kids who balk at olives: chop them fine so they melt into the sauce.
  • Mediterranean ✓ 4 of 4 with whole-wheat pasta: olive oil, fish, squash + tomato. light.
