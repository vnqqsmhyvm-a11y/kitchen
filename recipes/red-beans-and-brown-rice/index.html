Red Beans & Brown Rice

Budget Bytes' canned-bean red beans — trinity, smoked paprika, mild sausage — over brown rice. Kid-mild: skip the cayenne, use mild smoked sausage not hot andouille.

Ingredients

  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 14 oz mild smoked sausage, sliced (not hot andouille)
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 3 ribs celery, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 3 cans (15 oz) kidney beans
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 3 green onions, sliced
  • Brown rice, cooked, to serve (house rule: always brown — about 1.5 cups dry)

Method

  1. Brown the sausage in olive oil over medium heat, stirring, until the edges color.
  2. Add onion, bell pepper, celery, and garlic. Cook until the onion is soft.
  3. Stir in smoked paprika, oregano, thyme, garlic powder, onion powder, and black pepper. Cook 1 minute.
  4. Drain two cans of beans. Purée the third can (with its liquid) in a blender. Add drained beans, puréed beans, and broth to the pot. Stir.
  5. Cover, bring to a boil, then simmer medium-low 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. Taste; add salt only if it needs it (sausage + canned beans are already salty). Serve over brown rice with green onions.

Notes

  • Source already serves 6. The only household changes: no cayenne, mild sausage, brown rice instead of white.
  • Distinct from Brazilian Rice (pinto beans cooked into the rice with bay leaf) — this is creamy kidney beans spooned over rice, Cajun trinity, smoked paprika.
  • Adults who want heat: hot sauce at the table, not in the pot.
  • moderate + processed-meat: smoked sausage is the flavor, beans do the filling. Not a Mediterranean night.
