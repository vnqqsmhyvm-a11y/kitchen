Yellow Rice and Chicken

Ingredients

  • 2 packs Vigo yellow rice
  • 5 cups chicken broth
  • 2 bouillon cubes
  • 3 frozen chicken breasts

Method

  1. Add broth, rice, and bouillon cubes to the Instant Pot.
  2. Soak rice in broth for 10 minutes.
  3. Add chicken.
  4. Set cooker to the rice setting.
  5. Cook 12 minutes, regardless of the cooker changing the time.

Notes

  • Works straight from frozen chicken — good rushed-night meal.
