Greek Turkey & Rice Skillet

Budget Bytes' one-skillet Greek turkey and rice with spinach, sun-dried tomatoes, kalamata olives, oregano, lemon zest, and a little feta. Converted to brown rice. Dump-and-simmer — very little active time.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1.75 lb ground turkey (93–97% lean)
  • 1 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Black pepper
  • 1 1/2 cups long-grain brown rice, uncooked
  • 6 oz frozen cut-leaf spinach (no need to thaw)
  • 1/2 cup sliced sun-dried tomatoes
  • 3/4 cup sliced kalamata olives
  • 3 1/2 cups chicken broth (more than the source — brown rice)
  • Handful parsley, chopped
  • 1 1/2 lemons (zest + wedges)
  • 1 1/2 oz feta, crumbled

Method

  1. In a large, deep lidded skillet or Dutch oven, cook the olive oil and garlic over medium 1–2 minutes. Add turkey, oregano, salt, and pepper; cook until the turkey is done, ~5 minutes.
  2. Add the brown rice, frozen spinach, olives, and sun-dried tomatoes. Pour in the broth and stir well.
  3. Cover, bring to a boil over medium-high, then drop to the lowest heat that keeps a steady simmer. Cook 40 minutes.
  4. Quick stir, lid back on, heat off, rest 10 minutes. If the rice is still firm, add 1/4 cup hot broth and give it 5 more covered minutes.
  5. Top with lemon zest, parsley, and feta. Lemon wedges at the table.

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.5 from Budget Bytes' 19 oz turkey / 1 cup rice (serves 4–6).
  • Brown rice house rule: the source uses white rice with 1.5 cups broth and 15 minutes. Brown rice needs roughly double the liquid and ~40 minutes covered; broth and time above are adjusted. Check the first time.
  • Olives + sun-dried tomatoes also show up in Greek Turkey Burgers — buy once, use both.
  • Kids who dislike olives: slice them big so they're easy to pick out.
  • Mediterranean ✓ 4 of 4: olive oil, poultry, vegetables, brown rice. light (only 1.5 oz feta for the whole pan).
