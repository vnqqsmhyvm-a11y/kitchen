Sheet Pan Lemon Herb Chicken

One pan, one cleanup. Olive oil + lemon + herbs over chicken thighs and vegetables — textbook Mediterranean and very kid-friendly.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb bone-in chicken thighs (skin-on is fine — keeps it juicy)
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon oregano
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • Salt and pepper
  • Vegetables: baby potatoes halved, broccoli florets, or green beans (about 1 lb total)

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. Whisk olive oil, lemon, garlic, oregano, thyme, salt, pepper.
  3. Toss vegetables and chicken in the marinade.
  4. Spread on a sheet pan — chicken skin-side up.
  5. Roast 35–40 minutes until chicken is golden and vegetables are tender.

Notes

  • Genuinely 10 minutes of hands-on work.
  • Chicken thighs are budget-friendly and stay moist — buy in bulk at Sam's or Aldi.
  • A vegetable you add determines which health flags apply (broccoli → cruciferous).
