Enfrijoladas (Tortillas in Black Bean Sauce)

Budget Bytes' enfrijoladas: warm corn tortillas dipped in a smooth, smoky blended black bean sauce, folded around a little cheese and quick-pickled onion-cilantro, then baked and topped with more. A Mexican home-cooking staple — think enchiladas, but the sauce is the beans.

Ingredients

Black bean sauce

  • 3 cans (15 oz) black beans, drained
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika + 1/2 tsp chili powder (replaces the chipotles — see Notes)
  • 1/4 of a sweet or yellow onion, diced (rest goes in the topping)
  • 3/4 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/3 tsp garlic powder
  • 3 cups chicken or vegetable broth (start with 1 1/2)
  • Salt to taste

To assemble

  • 18 corn tortillas
  • The remaining onion (about 1 small), finely diced
  • 1/3 bunch cilantro, chopped
  • 6 oz shredded Monterey Jack, mozzarella, or crumbled queso fresco
  • Optional toppings: sour cream or Greek yogurt, avocado, salsa, a fried egg

Method

  1. Blend the beans, smoked paprika, chili powder, 1/4 onion, cumin, and garlic powder, streaming in broth until you have a smooth, thick (pourable but not watery) sauce. Salt to taste.
  2. Heat the oven to 350°F. Warm the tortillas (microwave the stack 30 seconds, or toast in a dry skillet) and keep covered.
  3. Toss the diced onion and cilantro with a pinch of salt.
  4. Spread a little sauce in a 9x13 dish; pour more into a wide shallow bowl for dipping.
  5. Dip each tortilla to coat both sides, sprinkle a little cheese and onion-cilantro on one half, fold in half, then in half again into a triangle. Shingle into the dish. Keep the filling light so they fold.
  6. Pour the remaining sauce over and bake ~15 minutes until heated through. Top with the rest of the cheese and onion-cilantro.

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.5 from Budget Bytes' 2 cans / 12 tortillas / serves 4.
  • Kid heat / one-off: the original blends 2 chipotles in adobo into the sauce — too spicy for the kids and it leaves an open can. Smoked paprika + chili powder gives the smoky note; blend in one chipotle for an adults' batch if you keep them on hand.
  • Latin-but-not-tacos night. Add a fried egg on top for extra protein (very traditional).
  • Not Sweet Potato Black Bean Enchiladas — no filling to speak of, bean sauce instead of red sauce, corn tortillas. Not Black Bean Tacos.
  • Mediterranean ✗ honestly: legumes + corn tortillas (whole grain) = 2 of 4 — onion is a garnish, no olive oil. Add a big salad and it's a balanced night anyway. light.
