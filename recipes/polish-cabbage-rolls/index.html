Polish Cabbage Rolls (Gołąbki)

RecipeTin Eats' gołąbki: blanched cabbage leaves wrapped around a pork, rice, and chopped-cabbage filling, simmered in stock, then finished in a buttery tomato sauce. A Sunday-afternoon-style project for a Saturday — the one weekend-project card in this batch's casserole/comfort lane.

Ingredients

Cabbage

  • 1 head green or savoy cabbage (~3 lb)
  • 1 Tbsp kosher salt (for the water)
  • 4 cups chicken broth

Filling

  • 1.5 lb ground pork (not extra-lean)
  • 1 large onion, peeled, left whole for grating
  • 1 1/2 cups panko (or regular breadcrumbs)
  • 2 cups cooked brown rice (house rule — source uses white)
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 Tbsp dried marjoram (or 1 1/2 tsp dried oregano)
  • 2 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 Tbsp chopped parsley
  • 3 cups packed finely chopped cabbage (from the same head)

Tomato sauce

  • 24 oz tomato purée/passata
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt, 1/4 tsp pepper, 1/2 tsp sugar
  • 2 Tbsp unsalted butter
  • Parsley to finish

Method

  1. Cabbage: Remove the ragged outer leaves and cut out the core in a cone. Boil a big pot of salted water, lower the cabbage in core-side down, cover, and simmer 10 minutes. Lift out and cool 10 minutes.
  2. Peel off 10–12 good leaves. Lay each flat and shave the thick rib flush with the leaf. Finely chop the remaining cabbage and squeeze out excess water.
  3. Filling: Grate the onion into a big bowl, stir in the panko, and let soak 1 minute. Add the pork, brown rice, eggs, garlic, marjoram, salt, pepper, parsley, and 3 packed cups of chopped cabbage. Mix by hand. Divide into 10–12 portions.
  4. Roll: Shape each portion into a ~3.5-inch log on the lower third of a leaf, roll up, fold in the sides halfway, and finish seam-side down.
  5. Simmer: Pour 1 cup broth into a large deep 12-inch pan, pack in the rolls seam-down, and pour over the rest of the broth. Simmer, covered, over medium 25 minutes (160°F inside). Lift the rolls out.
  6. Sauce: Boil the broth down to about 1 1/2 cups (~7 minutes). Stir in passata, tomato paste, salt, pepper, and sugar; simmer 5 minutes until like a thin pasta sauce. Stir in butter.
  7. Return the rolls seam-down, brush their tops with sauce, cover, and warm 5 minutes. Parsley on top.

Notes

  • RecipeTin's recipe is written for 5–6 (10 rolls, 700 g pork). Bumped pork to 1.5 lb and aim for 11–12 rolls so it's a solid 6; serve with mashed potatoes or rye bread for the big eaters.
  • Brown rice house rule: cook the brown rice ahead (or use leftover) — it goes into the filling already cooked.
  • Weekend project: ~60 minutes of hands-on rolling. Freezes beautifully — double and freeze a pan for a future rushed night.
  • Pork counts toward the ≤2 red/processed cap. Mediterranean ✗ (pork, butter, breadcrumbs). moderate.
