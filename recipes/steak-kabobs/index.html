A family grilled-steak favorite in skewer form — easier to portion for a crowd, and the vegetables make it a proper dinner.

Ingredients

Marinade

  • 2 lb sirloin or top round steak, cut into 1.5" chunks
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • Cracked pepper

Vegetables

  • Bell peppers, cut into chunks
  • Red onion, cut into chunks
  • Mushrooms (whole or halved)
  • Optional: cherry tomatoes

Method

  1. Mix marinade. Toss beef chunks in it. Marinate at least 1 hour (overnight best).
  2. Thread beef and vegetables onto skewers.
  3. Grill over high heat, 8–12 minutes total, turning every 3 minutes. Medium = ~10 min; medium-rare = ~8 min.
  4. Rest 3 minutes before serving.

Notes

  • Counts against the red-meat cap (≤2/week) — don't run it the same week as Pork Ribs or a heavy beef Chili night.
  • Cheaper cuts (top round, sirloin flap) work well marinated overnight — save the ribeye for standalone steaks.
