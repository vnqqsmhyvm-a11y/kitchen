Slow Cooker Black Bean Soup

Budget Bytes' dump-and-go black bean soup: a pound of dried black beans, onion, celery, grated carrot, garlic, salsa, chili powder, cumin, and oregano cooked in broth all day, then blended thick and creamy. Costs almost nothing, and the toppings bar makes it a kid dinner.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb dried black beans, rinsed and picked over
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 2 celery ribs, diced
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 cup mild salsa (jarred)
  • 1 Tbsp chili powder
  • 1/2 Tbsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 4 cups vegetable or chicken broth
  • 2 cups water
  • Salt to taste
  • Toppings bar: plain Greek yogurt or sour cream, shredded cheddar, diced avocado, cilantro, lime wedges, crushed tortilla chips
  • Optional base: a scoop of brown rice in each bowl (house rule: brown)

Method

  1. Put the garlic, onion, celery, carrots, beans, salsa, chili powder, cumin, oregano, broth, and water in a 5–7 qt slow cooker. Stir.
  2. Cover and cook on High 6–8 hours until the beans are very soft.
  3. Blend with an immersion blender until thick and creamy, leaving some beans whole. (Or blend 3–4 cups in a blender and stir back in.)
  4. Taste and salt as needed. Serve with the toppings bar.

Notes

  • Budget Bytes' recipe serves 6 as written — no scaling.
  • No soak needed — High for 6–8 hours softens dried beans. Use High, not Low; old beans may take the full 8.
  • Mild salsa + 1 Tbsp chili powder is kid-level. Hot salsa at the table for adults.
  • Freeze in 2-cup containers for lunches.
  • Not Black Bean Tacos or any of the chilis — a blended soup, no meat, no tomatoes beyond the salsa.
  • Mediterranean ✓ with the brown rice base: legumes + vegetables + whole grain = 3 of 4 (no olive oil). Without rice it drops to 2 of 4. light — watch the cheese/sour cream toppings.
