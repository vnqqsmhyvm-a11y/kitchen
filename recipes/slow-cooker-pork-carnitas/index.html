Slow Cooker Pork Carnitas

Budget Bytes' carnitas: cubed pork butt rubbed with cumin, oregano, and a little chipotle, slow-cooked on onion, garlic, cinnamon stick, and orange juice + zest, then shredded and crisped in a skillet. Taco/burrito-bowl night that isn't ground beef, and the leftovers are better than the first night.

Ingredients

  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, peeled
  • 2 small cinnamon sticks
  • 1/2 Tbsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 Tbsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Freshly cracked pepper
  • 1/4–1/2 tsp chipotle powder (or smoked paprika for kids)
  • 3 lb pork butt (shoulder) roast, cut into 2-inch cubes
  • 2 small oranges (zest strips from one, ~1/2 cup juice total)
  • To serve: corn or flour tortillas, or brown rice + black beans (house rule: always brown); onion, cilantro, lime, salsa, avocado

Method

  1. Put the onion, garlic, and cinnamon sticks in the slow cooker.
  2. Mix oregano, cumin, salt, pepper, and chipotle. Toss the pork cubes in the spice mix and pile on top of the onions.
  3. Peel the zest from one orange in strips with a vegetable peeler. Squeeze ~1/2 cup juice over the meat; scatter the zest on top.
  4. Cover; cook on high 5 hours (or low ~8) until it falls apart.
  5. Shred with tongs; discard the cinnamon sticks.
  6. Crisp it: move meat plus a splash of liquid to a nonstick or cast-iron skillet over medium and let it brown on the edges without stirring much. Do it in batches.
  7. Serve as tacos or burrito bowls.

Notes

  • Budget Bytes' 3 lb roast "serves 8" — kept as-is: dinner for 6 plus a burrito/nacho round of leftovers.
  • Pork butt is a Sam's staple and among the cheapest meats per pound. Trim the big fat cap before cubing.
  • Kid heat: chipotle powder is the only heat — use 1/4 tsp or swap smoked paprika.
  • Not Slow Cooker Mexican Shredded Beef: pork, citrus + cinnamon, crisped in a skillet; no tomato or chipotles in adobo.
  • Pork counts toward the ≤2 red/processed cap. Mediterranean ✗. moderate — fatty cut; skim the liquid.
