Slow Cooker Corned Beef

Ingredients

  • 1 corned beef brisket (~3 lb), with its seasoning packet if included
  • 1 cup water or beef broth
  • Instant mashed potatoes (on hand) — to serve
  • Canned green beans (on hand) — to serve
  • Optional, if buying: 1 small cabbage, cut into wedges; 3-4 carrots, chunked

Method

  1. Place brisket fat-side up in the slow cooker, sprinkle with seasoning packet, add water/broth.
  2. Cook on Low 8 hours (or High 5 hours) until fork-tender.
  3. If using cabbage/carrots, add them for the last 1-1.5 hours of cook time.
  4. Slice against the grain. Serve with mashed potatoes and green beans.

Notes

  • First time making this — capture real timing/seasoning notes after.
