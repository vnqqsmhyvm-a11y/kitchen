White Bean & Kale Soup

Creamy cannellini soup with rosemary, garlic, and a bag of kale wilted in at the end — Budget Bytes' pantry method plus their own kale add-in. A real legume dinner that isn't chili, tacos, or bolognese.

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 cans (15 oz) cannellini beans
  • 3 cups vegetable or chicken broth
  • 1/2 tsp dried rosemary
  • 1/4 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 pinch crushed red pepper (skip for kids)
  • Freshly cracked black pepper
  • 1 bag (8–10 oz) chopped kale (or baby spinach)
  • 1–2 Tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt, to taste
  • Brown rice, cooked, to serve (house rule: always brown) — optional but makes it a fuller plate
  • Optional: grated Parmesan

Method

  1. Pour one can of cannellini beans (with its liquid) into a blender and purée until smooth. Drain the other three cans.
  2. Sauté the onion in olive oil over medium heat until soft, about 5 minutes. Add the garlic and cook 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Add the puréed beans, drained beans, broth, rosemary, thyme, crushed red pepper, and black pepper. Stir to combine.
  4. Cover, bring to a boil, then uncover and simmer 15 minutes, stirring occasionally. Smash some beans against the pot to thicken.
  5. Stir in the kale and cook 3–5 minutes until wilted. Finish with lemon juice. Taste and add salt if the broth needs it.
  6. Serve in bowls, with a scoop of brown rice if you want a starch, and Parmesan if you have it.

Notes

  • Scaled from Budget Bytes' 4-serving rosemary-garlic white bean soup (3 cans beans / 2 cups broth) to 4 cans + extra broth for 6; kale and onion are their documented add-ins, not a vibe invention.
  • Spinach stands in for kale if the kids balk — stir it in off heat, it wilts in a minute.
  • Freezes well. Reheat with a splash of broth; the beans thicken in the fridge.
  • Mediterranean ✓: olive oil, legumes over red meat, real vegetables, brown rice if you serve it.
