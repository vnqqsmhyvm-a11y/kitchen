RecipeTin Eats' huevos rancheros: a quick chunky tomato-black bean-bell pepper sauce with cumin and paprika, eggs poached right in it, spooned into charred tortilla "bowls" and topped with avocado, cheese, and sour cream. Breakfast-for-dinner in 25 minutes.

Ingredients

Tortilla bowls

  • 6–8 (8-inch) whole-wheat or flour tortillas

Tomato-bean sauce

  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 small onions, halved and sliced
  • 2 cups diced red bell pepper (~2)
  • 1/2 cup chopped cilantro, divided
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained (about 1 1/2 cups)
  • 1 tsp paprika + 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1–1 1/2 tsp salt, pepper

Eggs

  • 8–10 eggs (1 per kid, 2 for anyone hungry)

Toppings: diced avocado, sour cream or Greek yogurt, shredded cheese, hot sauce for adults

Method

  1. Bowls: char each tortilla in a dry skillet over high heat, ~30 seconds per side. Press into a cereal bowl while warm; it holds the shape as it cools.
  2. Sauce: heat the oil in a 12-inch skillet with a lid over high. Sauté the garlic and onion 2 minutes; add the bell pepper and cook 2 minutes until starting to char.
  3. Add the tomatoes, beans, paprika, cumin, salt, pepper, and most of the cilantro. Simmer 1–2 minutes to thicken, then turn to medium.
  4. Eggs: make 8–10 wells and crack in the eggs. Cover and cook ~3 minutes (check at 2) until whites set and yolks are how you like them. If they won't fit, do two rounds or two skillets.
  5. Spoon sauce and eggs into the tortilla bowls; top with cilantro, avocado, cheese, and sour cream.

Notes

  • Scaled ×2 from RecipeTin Eats' serves 3 (3 tortillas, 3 eggs), with extra eggs so the teens get two.
  • Whole-wheat tortillas for the whole-grain point; Nagi uses flour.
  • Not Shakshuka — Mexican flavors (cumin, cilantro, black beans), quick-simmered, in tortilla bowls. Not Black Bean Tacos.
  • Kid-mild as written; hot sauce at the table.
  • Mediterranean ✓: olive oil, eggs + beans, peppers/tomato, whole-wheat tortillas — 4 of 4. light (watch the toppings).
