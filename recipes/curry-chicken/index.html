Curry Chicken (Red Curry Chicken & Rice)

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb boneless chicken, sliced into bite-size pieces (or use grilled-and-ready chicken strips — Tyson 22 oz bag)
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced thin (or carrots if you prefer)
  • 1 bunch green onions, chopped
  • 1 jar or can (4 oz) red curry paste
  • 2 cans (14 oz) coconut milk
  • ½ bunch cilantro, chopped
  • 3 TB brown sugar
  • A little coconut oil or butter (for browning)
  • Brown rice, cooked, to serve

Method

  1. If using raw chicken: slice into bite-size pieces and brown along with the green onions in a little coconut oil or butter.
  2. Add the rest of the ingredients except the cilantro and rice.
  3. Instant Pot: cook 4 minutes, quick-release the pressure, then stir in the cilantro.
  4. Stovetop: simmer 15–20 minutes on low, stirring frequently; turn off the heat and add the cilantro.
  5. Serve over rice.
  6. Thicker sauce (optional): mix 1–2 TBS cornstarch with a little water, stir in, and simmer a few minutes.

Notes

  • Reheats well — premakeable.
  • House rule: rice is always brown rice.
  • Card says "cook your favorite rice"; original card kept in the kitchen.
