Slow Cooker Lentil Soup

Dump-and-go brown lentil soup — tomato paste, cumin, thyme, carrots. Morning load, dinner's done. Not chili, not chana, not lentil bolognese (that's a pasta sauce).

Ingredients

  • 1 lb dried brown lentils, sorted and rinsed
  • 2 medium carrots, chopped
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 8 cups chicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 can (6 oz) tomato paste
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil (stirred in at the end, or drizzle on bowls)
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Brown rice, cooked, to serve (house rule: always brown) — optional, makes it a fuller dinner
  • Optional: lemon wedges, a handful of spinach stirred in at serving

Method

  1. Spray a 5- to 6-quart slow cooker. Add the lentils, carrots, onion, garlic, broth, tomato paste, cumin, and thyme. Stir until the tomato paste dissolves.
  2. Cover and cook on Low 8–10 hours, until the lentils are tender. (High ~4 hours if you need it faster — check at 3.5.)
  3. Stir well. The soup thickens as it sits; add a splash of broth if it's too thick. Season with salt and pepper. Stir in the olive oil.
  4. Serve in bowls, over brown rice if you want a grain, with lemon if you have it.

Notes

  • Betty Crocker lists "1/2 cup dry sherry or broth" — use extra broth. No reason to buy sherry for one soup.
  • Source serves 7; this is already household-sized. Leftovers freeze. Lentils keep drinking liquid — loosen with broth on reheat.
  • Distinct from Lentil Bolognese (red lentils cooked into a pasta sauce) and from Slow Cooker Chana Saag (chickpeas + spinach + garam masala).
  • Mediterranean ✓: olive oil finish, legumes, tomato paste, vegetables, brown rice if served.
