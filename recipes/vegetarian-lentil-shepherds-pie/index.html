Vegetarian Lentil Shepherd's Pie

Budget Bytes' vegetarian shepherd's pie: carrots, celery, mushrooms, onion, and peas in a thyme-and-smoked-paprika gravy with lentils, topped with mashed potatoes and baked until golden. Classic casserole comfort without the ground meat.

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup dried brown lentils (makes ~2 cups cooked) — or 2 cups canned, rinsed
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 carrots, peeled and diced
  • 2 celery ribs, diced
  • 8 oz button mushrooms, sliced
  • 3/4 tsp salt, 1 tsp dried thyme, 1/2 tsp smoked paprika, pepper
  • 1 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 Tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1 1/2 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 4 cups mashed potatoes (~2 1/2 lb potatoes mashed with a little milk and butter, or a Sam's/Aldi refrigerated tub)

Method

  1. Simmer the lentils in plenty of water 20–25 minutes until tender; drain. (Skip if using canned.)
  2. Sauté the onion and garlic in olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet over medium, 3–5 minutes.
  3. Add the carrots and celery; sauté 5 minutes. Add the mushrooms, salt, thyme, smoked paprika, and pepper; cook until the mushrooms soften, 3–5 minutes.
  4. Stir in the tomato paste and flour; cook 2 minutes until pasty.
  5. Add the broth, scraping up the bottom; simmer until slightly thick. Stir in the lentils and peas.
  6. Heat the oven to 400°F. Spread the mashed potatoes over the filling (in the skillet or a 9x13) and rake the top with a fork.
  7. Bake 15 minutes until hot; broil 2–3 minutes to brown the top, watching closely.

Notes

  • Budget Bytes' recipe serves 6. Tweak: the lentils are listed as optional (1 cup cooked); they're required here and doubled to 2 cups so this is a real legume dinner. Broth bumped from 1 to 1 1/2 cups to keep the extra lentils saucy.
  • Not Healthier Hamburger Helper or Chicken and Stuffing Casserole — meatless, potato-topped. First shepherd's pie in the deck.
  • Mushrooms can be chopped small for picky kids — they disappear into the gravy.
  • Mediterranean ✓ (olive oil, legumes, lots of vegetables; potatoes rather than whole grain — 3 of 4). moderate for the buttered mash.
