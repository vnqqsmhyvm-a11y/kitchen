Smoky Potato Chickpea Stew

Budget Bytes' smoky potato chickpea stew: potatoes, chickpeas, and fire-roasted tomatoes simmered in broth with curry powder and smoked paprika, then smashed to thicken and finished with kale. Thick, warming, vegan, and almost all pantry.

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 yellow onion, finely diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger (or jar)
  • 1 Tbsp curry powder
  • 1 Tbsp smoked paprika
  • Pinch crushed red pepper (optional; adults can add at the table)
  • 1 can (15 oz) fire-roasted diced tomatoes
  • 2 russet potatoes (1 3/4–2 lb), peeled, 1-inch cubes
  • 2 cans (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed (see Notes)
  • 4 1/2 cups vegetable or chicken broth
  • 1/4 lb fresh kale, chopped (or a few handfuls of spinach)
  • Salt to taste
  • To serve: crusty whole-grain bread

Method

  1. Sauté the onion, garlic, and ginger in the olive oil in a large pot over medium until soft and translucent, ~5 minutes.
  2. Add the curry powder, smoked paprika, and red pepper; stir 1–2 minutes to toast.
  3. Add the potatoes, tomatoes with juices, and chickpeas. Pour in the broth and stir.
  4. Cover, bring to a boil over medium-high, then simmer on low with the lid on for 45 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  5. Stir well and smash some potatoes against the side of the pot to thicken. Stir in the kale until wilted. Season with salt.
  6. Serve with crusty bread.

Notes

  • Budget Bytes' recipe already serves 6. Tweak: one can of chickpeas doubled to two (and a splash more broth) so it holds up as a protein-forward dinner for six instead of a side-ish soup.
  • Kid heat: red pepper flakes moved to the table; curry powder + smoked paprika are warm, not hot.
  • Not Slow Cooker Lentil Soup or White Bean Kale Soup — chickpeas and potato, smoky-curry flavor, thick enough to be a stew.
  • Mediterranean ✓ (olive oil, legumes, vegetables, whole-grain bread). light.
