Parmesan-Crusted Baked Tilapia

Mild white fish under a golden parmesan crust — crispy, kid-friendly, and on the table in about 25 minutes. The tilapia option for a tilapia-swap week (see Diet Lens).

Ingredients

  • 2 lb tilapia fillets (about 6)
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 3 Tbsp butter, softened
  • 2 Tbsp fresh lemon juice (about 1 lemon)
  • 1/4 tsp dried basil
  • 1/4 tsp paprika
  • 1/4 tsp seasoned salt
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • Optional: 1/4 cup Italian panko for extra crunch
  • Fresh parsley or chives, to garnish

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Line a baking sheet with foil or parchment and lightly grease.
  2. Pat the tilapia dry, arrange on the sheet, and season with the seasoned salt and pepper.
  3. Mix the parmesan, mayonnaise, softened butter, lemon juice, basil, and paprika (and panko, if using) into a paste.
  4. Bake the tilapia 8–10 minutes until nearly cooked through.
  5. Spread the parmesan mixture over the tops and return to the oven — or broil — 3–5 minutes until golden, bubbly, and the fish flakes easily. Garnish and serve.

Notes

  • Fish-free swap: give the fish-free the same coating on a chicken breast from the bag — bake longer (~20–25 min to 165°F). The fish-free fish-night fallback.
  • Lean white fish — no omega-3. Does NOT count toward the fatty-fish quota; this is a deliberate tilapia-swap week per the Diet Lens Tilapia Swap rule.
  • moderate tier: mayo + butter + parmesan crust over an otherwise lean fillet.
