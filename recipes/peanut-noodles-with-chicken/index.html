Peanut Noodles with Chicken

Budget Bytes' 25-minute peanut noodles — a creamy peanut-coconut-lime sauce with chicken bites, tossed with spaghetti or lo mein noodles. Kid-friendly Asian noodle night; sriracha lives at the table.

Ingredients

Peanut sauce

  • 1/2 cup smooth natural peanut butter
  • 1 Tbsp sriracha in the pot (source uses 2 Tbsp for 4 — see Notes), more at the table
  • 3 Tbsp brown sugar
  • 3 Tbsp lime juice (about 1 1/2 limes)
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 1/2 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 1/2 cans (13.5 oz) coconut milk (light is fine)

Noodles

  • 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast, 1/2–1 inch pieces
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp oil
  • 12 oz noodles (spaghetti, linguine, or lo mein)
  • 1 bag (12 oz) frozen stir-fry vegetables or broccoli florets (house add — see Notes)
  • 1/3 cup chopped peanuts
  • 1/3 cup chopped cilantro

Method

  1. Whisk the peanut butter, sriracha, brown sugar, soy sauce, lime juice, garlic, and ginger. Whisk in the coconut milk until smooth.
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high with the oil. Brown the chicken on all sides — don't overcook.
  3. Pour in the peanut sauce, lower to medium-low, and scrape up the browned bits. Simmer while the noodles cook.
  4. Boil the noodles per the package; add the frozen vegetables to the pot for the last 2–3 minutes. Drain.
  5. Toss noodles and vegetables into the skillet with the chicken and sauce. Top with peanuts and cilantro.

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.5 from Budget Bytes' serves-4 (2/3 lb chicken / 8 oz noodles); chicken raised to a full 1 lb so it's a real protein portion.
  • Kid heat: half the sriracha in the pot. Beth's version is noticeably spicy.
  • House add: a bag of frozen stir-fry veg or broccoli in the noodle water so there's a vegetable — not in the source.
  • Peanut allergy check if friends are over.
  • Mediterranean ✗: refined noodles, coconut milk + peanut butter as the fat. moderate.
