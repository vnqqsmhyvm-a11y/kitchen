Chunky Ham & White Bean Soup

Budget Bytes' thick, chunky ham and bean soup — browned ham, carrots, celery, onion, and cannellini beans (one can puréed for body) in a thyme broth. Uses the leftover holiday ham, or a cheap ham steak any other week.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 yellow onions, diced
  • 4–5 carrots, sliced
  • 4–5 ribs celery, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1.5 lb cooked ham (leftover ham or ham steaks), bite-size dice
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 4 cans (15 oz) cannellini beans — 1 can puréed with its liquid, 3 drained (source ratio rounded up)
  • 1/3 tsp dried thyme
  • Freshly cracked black pepper
  • 3 cups chicken broth, plus more as needed

Method

  1. Brown the ham in the oil in a large soup pot over medium, 3–5 minutes. Scoop it into a bowl.
  2. Add the onion, carrots, celery, and garlic to the same pot and sauté ~5 minutes, letting their moisture lift the browned bits.
  3. Meanwhile, blend 1 can of beans with its liquid until smooth. Drain the other cans.
  4. Add all the beans, the thyme, plenty of pepper, and the broth. Bring to a boil over medium-high.
  5. Boil on medium 15 minutes, stirring now and then, until thickened. Add broth if it gets too thick.
  6. Stir the ham back in. Taste — ham is salty, so you likely won't need salt. Serve with crusty bread.

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.5 from Budget Bytes' serves-4 (1 lb ham / 3 cans beans / 2 cups broth); rounded to 4 cans beans so the legume portion is generous.
  • Freezes very well.
  • Ham is processed meat — counts toward the ≤2 red/processed cap even though it's a light, bean-heavy soup.
  • Not White Bean & Kale Soup (vegetarian, rosemary, kale) — this one is ham-and-root-vegetable and much thicker.
  • Mediterranean ✗ (processed meat as the flavor base; 2 of 4 with olive oil + legumes/vegetables). light.
