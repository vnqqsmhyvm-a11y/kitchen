Black-Eyed Peas and Greens

Budget Bytes' quickie black-eyed peas and greens: canned black-eyed peas simmered with onion, garlic, oregano, and smoked paprika in broth, smashed a little to thicken, with spinach wilted in at the end. A Southern-style pot of peas in 35 minutes — serve over brown rice with a wedge of cornbread.

Ingredients

  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 1/2 yellow onions, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 4 cans (15 oz) black-eyed peas, drained
  • 3/4 tsp dried oregano
  • 3/4 tsp smoked paprika
  • Pinch cayenne (optional; hot sauce at the table instead)
  • 1/3 tsp black pepper
  • 3 cups vegetable or chicken broth
  • 6 oz fresh spinach (or chopped frozen collards/kale)
  • Salt to taste
  • To serve: brown rice (house rule), cornbread (optional), hot sauce

Method

  1. Sauté the onion and garlic in olive oil in a large pot over medium until soft and translucent, ~5 minutes.
  2. Add the black-eyed peas, oregano, smoked paprika, cayenne, pepper, and broth. Stir.
  3. Cover, bring to a boil over medium-high, then simmer on medium-low 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  4. Smash some peas against the side of the pot (or blend a little with an immersion blender) to thicken the broth.
  5. Stir in the spinach until wilted. Taste and add salt.
  6. Serve over brown rice, with cornbread and hot sauce.

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.5 from Budget Bytes' 3 cans / serves 4; peas rounded to 4 cans (from 4 1/2) since it goes over brown rice.
  • Kid heat: cayenne optional; smoked paprika gives the "ham hock" smokiness without meat.
  • Black-eyed peas: Bush's or store brand canned — both Sam's and Aldi carry them. Good New Year's Day dinner.
  • Not Red Beans and Brown Rice — no sausage, greens in the pot.
  • Mediterranean ✓ (olive oil, legumes, greens, brown rice — 4 of 4). light.
