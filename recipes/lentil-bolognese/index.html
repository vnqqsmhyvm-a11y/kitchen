Lentil Bolognese

Looks and eats like regular bolognese, costs a fraction. Red lentils dissolve into the sauce — kids rarely notice. Hits legumes and tomato in one pot.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup red lentils (dried — very cheap, available everywhere)
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 2 cups vegetable or chicken broth
  • Salt and pepper
  • Pasta to serve

Method

  1. Sauté onion and garlic in olive oil over medium heat until soft.
  2. Add lentils, crushed tomatoes, broth, and seasoning.
  3. Simmer 25–30 minutes until lentils are dissolved and sauce is thick.
  4. Serve over pasta. Top with Parmesan if desired.

Notes

  • Red lentils require zero soaking — straight from the bag.
  • A very cheap week-saver: lentils + canned tomatoes + pasta ≈ $6–8 for 8 servings.
  • The family's "vegetarian night" option without feeling like a sacrifice.
