Turkey Keema Curry

olive magazine's kheema matar — ground turkey and peas simmered with cumin, coriander, turmeric, ginger, and garlic in stock, finished with cilantro and lemon. Served with a quick tomato-onion-ginger salad. Ground-meat Indian dinner from pantry spices; the chilies are optional so kids can eat it.

Ingredients

Keema

  • 1 1/2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1.5 lb ground turkey (about 750 g)
  • 1 1/2 onions, finely chopped
  • 4–5 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1 1/2 thumb-size pieces ginger, finely chopped (or 1 1/2 tsp ground)
  • 1–3 green chiles (jalapeño works), finely chopped — optional, see Notes
  • 1 Tbsp ground cumin
  • 1 Tbsp ground coriander
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
  • 2 1/2 cups (600 ml) chicken stock
  • 12 oz (375 g) frozen peas
  • Small bunch cilantro, chopped
  • Juice of 3/4 lemon
  • Salt and pepper

Tomato salad

  • 10 oz cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • Juice of 3/4 lemon
  • Thumb of ginger, finely grated

To serve: brown rice (house rule: always brown) and/or warm rotis or naan

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over high heat. Cook the turkey 5 minutes, breaking it up, until it starts to brown and the liquid has cooked off.
  2. Lower the heat, add the onion with a pinch of salt, and cook 5 minutes until softening. Add garlic, ginger, and chiles (if using) and cook 5 minutes more.
  3. Stir in the cumin, coriander, and turmeric; cook 2 minutes.
  4. Pour in the stock and simmer 20 minutes.
  5. Add the peas and cook 5 minutes. Stir in cilantro and lemon juice; season.
  6. Toss the salad ingredients with salt and pepper. Serve the keema with brown rice or rotis and the salad alongside.

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.5 from olive's serves-4 (500 g turkey / 250 g peas).
  • Kid heat: the source uses 2 green chilies for 4. For this family, cook the base without them and stir a chopped jalapeño into the adults' portion — or 1 seeded chile in the whole pot at most.
  • Swapped vegetable oil for olive oil (house Mediterranean nudge).
  • Not Curry Chicken (Thai coconut) or Slow Cooker Chana Saag (chickpea-spinach); this is a dry-ish ground-meat curry like a sloppy-joe filling.
  • Mediterranean ✓: olive oil, poultry, peas + tomato salad, brown rice. light.
