Baked Cod with Tomatoes

Cod baked under garlic-oregano tomatoes — Budget Bytes' pantry method, specified as cod so it isn't another tilapia clone. Same tomatoes over a chicken breast for the fish-allergic eater.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb cod fillets (thawed if frozen; pollock or haddock also fine — not tilapia)
  • 2 cans (15 oz) fire-roasted diced tomatoes, most of the liquid drained
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Freshly cracked pepper
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • Chopped parsley, optional
  • Brown rice, cooked, to serve (house rule: always brown)

Fish-free fallback (same sauce, same pan)

  • 1–2 boneless chicken breasts, from the bag

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Mix the drained tomatoes with garlic, olive oil, oregano, salt, pepper, and lemon juice.
  2. Spread about 1/3 of the tomato mixture in a large baking dish (or two dishes if needed so the fish isn't stacked).
  3. Nestle the cod in a single layer. Put the fish-free chicken at one end of the dish (or a small second dish). Spoon the remaining tomatoes over everything so the fish is covered.
  4. Bake 15–20 minutes until the cod flakes and hits 145°F. The chicken needs longer — leave it in until 165°F (~25 minutes total), or start it 10 minutes ahead of the fish.
  5. Serve the fish (and the fish-free chicken) with the pan tomatoes over brown rice.

Notes

  • Scaled from Budget Bytes (1 lb fish / 1 can tomatoes / serves 4) to 2 lb cod + 2 cans tomatoes for 6.
  • Not a tilapia clone. Different fish, tomato-herb bake — no parmesan crust, no honey-garlic glaze, no blackening rub. Those four tilapia cards stay on their own rotation.
  • Frozen cod from Sam's or Aldi is fine; thaw in the fridge.
  • Fish-free swap: same tomatoes, same oven, chicken breast. Don't put the fish-free chicken in the fish juices if you can help it — a foil divider or a small second dish is easiest.
  • Lean white fish — no omega-3. Does NOT count toward the fatty-fish quota. Use on a tilapia-swap week when you want variety without another tilapia recipe.
  • Mediterranean ✓: olive oil, fish, cooked tomato, brown rice.
