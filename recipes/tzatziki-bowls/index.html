Tzatziki Bowls

Greek-leaning taco bowls: seasoned ground beef, a fresh Greek salad, chickpeas, tzatziki, feta.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean ground beef
  • 3 Tbsp taco seasoning (spicy Siete brand recommended, any brand works)
  • 2 cups cucumber, halved and sliced (mini cucumbers work well)
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 small red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1/4 cup sliced olives (black or any variety)
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp red wine vinegar
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Pinch dried oregano, to taste
  • 1-2 cups canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup tzatziki
  • Crumbled feta, optional
  • Optional toppings: cubed avocado, pickled red onion, fresh dill or parsley, fresh lemon juice, hummus

Method

  1. Brown the meat: Heat a pan on the stove to medium. Add ground beef, leave untouched 2-3 minutes so the bottom browns. Add taco seasoning, cook and crumble until browned with no pink remaining. Turn off heat, set aside.
  2. Make the salad: In a mixing bowl, combine cucumber, tomatoes, red onion, bell pepper, olives, olive oil, red wine vinegar, salt, pepper, and oregano. Toss to combine, taste and adjust seasoning.
  3. Assemble bowls: Makes 4 bowls per the original card. To each bowl, add a portion of the cooked meat, the salad, the chickpeas, 1/4 cup tzatziki, and a sprinkle of feta. Finish with any optional toppings.

Notes

  • Card serves 4 — household is 6, so scale ~1.5x (1.5 lb beef, etc.) for a real dinner.
  • Light tier: lean beef, heavily vegetable-forward salad, olive oil dressing, yogurt-based tzatziki (not sour cream/mayo), only a light sprinkle of feta.
  • Does NOT clear mediterranean: true despite the Greek flavor profile — olive oil and real vegetables both hit, but beef (not fish/poultry/legumes) is the headline protein and there's no whole-ish carb component (no rice/potato/pasta base) — only 2 of 4 criteria met.
