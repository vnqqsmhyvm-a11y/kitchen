Creamy Pesto Pasta Salad

Cold pasta salad with rotisserie chicken, chickpeas, and a creamy pesto dressing.

Ingredients

Creamy Pesto Dressing

  • 1/3 cup pesto, store-bought
  • 1/3 cup finely grated parmesan (freshly grated, not powdered)
  • 1/4 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 Tbsp mayonnaise (light if desired)
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1/4 tsp salt, adjust to taste
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1 Tbsp water to thin, optional

Pasta Salad

  • 5 cups cooked and cooled pasta (protein pasta preferred)
  • 2 cups diced rotisserie chicken, skin and bones removed
  • 1 cup canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 2 cups halved cherry tomatoes
  • 1 cup marinated artichoke hearts, drained and chopped small
  • 1 cup mini bocconcini pearls, drained
  • 2 handfuls baby arugula
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Whisk together all Creamy Pesto Dressing ingredients until smooth.
  2. In a large bowl, combine all Pasta Salad ingredients.
  3. Toss the salad with the dressing. Taste and adjust seasoning.

Notes

  • No scaling needed — already sized for 6.
  • Doesn't clear mediterranean: true — chicken + chickpeas and real vegetables hit 2 of 4 criteria, but the dressing is mayo/yogurt-forward rather than olive-oil-primary, and "protein pasta" isn't necessarily whole grain.
