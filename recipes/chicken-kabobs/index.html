Chicken Kabobs

Skewers of marinated chicken and vegetables on the grill. Easy to customize per person — skip the peppers on one skewer for a picky kid, double the chicken on another.

Ingredients

Marinade

  • 2 lb boneless chicken thighs or breasts, cut into 1.5" chunks
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • Salt and pepper

Vegetables (pick 2–3)

  • Bell peppers (any color), cut into chunks
  • Red onion, cut into chunks
  • Zucchini, sliced thick
  • Cherry tomatoes

Method

  1. Mix marinade. Toss chicken in it. Marinate at least 30 minutes (overnight is better — makes this premakeable).
  2. Thread chicken and vegetables onto skewers, alternating.
  3. Grill over medium-high, 12–15 minutes total, turning every 3–4 minutes.
  4. Chicken is done when it reaches 165°F and has visible char.

Notes

  • Marinate the night before → 15-minute grill job the day of.
  • 2026-07 feedback: well-liked but ran short using 2 large breasts (~1.5 lb). Use the full 2 lb minimum; 2.5–3 lb when all 6 are home.
  • Bell peppers and cherry tomatoes count toward the Mediterranean profile.
  • If making alongside a fish night for the fish-allergic eater: put the fish-free chicken skewers on with the same marinade — the fish-free plate gets the same dinner as everyone minus the fish.
  • Skewers: metal reusable ones work well; wooden ones need a 30-min water soak first.
