Tuna Noodle Casserole

Budget Bytes' from-scratch tuna noodle casserole — no canned soup: a butter-flour-milk sauce with cream cheese, sautéed mushrooms and onion, peas, egg noodles, and chunk light tuna under a Parmesan crumb top. Pantry comfort casserole; assembles ahead.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 yellow onions, finely diced
  • 12 oz mushrooms, sliced
  • 3/4 cup frozen peas
  • 12 oz wide egg noodles
  • 4 cans (5 oz) chunk light tuna, drained (~18–20 oz)
  • 6 Tbsp butter, divided
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt, divided; 1/3 tsp black pepper
  • 3 Tbsp flour
  • 3 cups milk
  • 1 1/2 cups vegetable or chicken broth
  • 6 oz cream cheese
  • 3 Tbsp breadcrumbs
  • 3 Tbsp grated Parmesan

Fish-free fallback (same sauce)

  • 1 cup shredded rotisserie or canned chicken
  • A small loaf pan or 1-qt baking dish

Method

  1. Sauté the onion in 1 1/2 Tbsp butter over medium 3–5 minutes. Add the mushrooms with a little salt and pepper; cook ~5 minutes. Scoop out and set aside.
  2. Melt 3 Tbsp butter in the skillet, add the flour, and whisk ~1 minute while it bubbles.
  3. Whisk in the milk, broth, and cream cheese; cook over medium, whisking, until the cream cheese melts and the sauce simmers and thickens, ~5 minutes.
  4. Stir in the peas, mushrooms, and onions; season with the remaining salt.
  5. Heat oven to 350°F. Boil the noodles ~7 minutes until just tender; drain.
  6. Melt the last 1 1/2 Tbsp butter and stir in the breadcrumbs and Parmesan.
  7. The fish-free dish first: mix ~1 1/2 cups noodles and a generous cup of sauce with the fish-free chicken in the small pan.
  8. Combine the remaining sauce, noodles, and drained tuna in a 9×13 (or 3–4 qt) dish. Top both dishes with the crumbs.
  9. Bake ~25 minutes until hot and golden.

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.5 from Budget Bytes' 9×9 version (12 oz tuna, 8 oz noodles, "6 servings" of 1.3 cups) — those are small portions for this crew.
  • Chunk light tuna is the lower-mercury, cheaper option. Tuna is lean-ish — doesn't reliably count as fatty fish (salmon and sardines do).
  • Fish-free swap: the fish-free mini casserole is assembled from sauce and noodles before the tuna is added.
  • Make-ahead: assemble the night before, refrigerate, add ~10 minutes to the bake.
  • heavy: butter roux + cream cheese + egg noodles — cream-based casserole. Count it as the week's one heavy night. Mediterranean ✗.
