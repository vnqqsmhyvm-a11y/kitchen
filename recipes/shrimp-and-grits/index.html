Shrimp and Grits

Budget Bytes' shrimp and grits: cheesy grits cooked in broth, topped with Cajun-seared shrimp in a quick onion-garlic pan gravy. Lowcountry comfort in 35 minutes, and a Southern night that isn't jambalaya or red beans.

Ingredients

Grits

  • 3 cups water
  • 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 1/2 cups old-fashioned grits (not instant)
  • 1/3 tsp salt + 1/3 tsp pepper
  • 3 Tbsp butter
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded sharp cheddar

Shrimp and gravy

  • 1 1/2 lb large shrimp, thawed, peeled, deveined
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp mild Cajun or Creole seasoning (check it isn't extra-hot)
  • 2 Tbsp vegetable oil, divided
  • 3/4 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 Tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1 1/2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1/3 cup milk
  • 1 tsp hot sauce (more at the table for adults)
  • 3 green onions, sliced

Fish-free fallback (same seasoning, same gravy)

  • 1 boneless chicken breast, cut into bite-size pieces

Method

  1. Bring the water, 3 cups broth, salt, and pepper to a boil. Whisk in the grits, cover, and cook on low 15–20 minutes, stirring now and then.
  2. Pat the shrimp dry and season with most of the Cajun seasoning; season the fish-free chicken with the rest on a separate plate.
  3. The fish-free chicken first: heat 1 tsp oil in the gravy skillet over medium-high and cook the fish-free chicken 5–6 minutes until 165°F. Move it to a small bowl.
  4. In the same skillet, add 1 Tbsp oil and the onion; cook 5 minutes. Add garlic for 1 minute, sprinkle in the flour, and cook 1 minute.
  5. Whisk in the 1 1/2 cups broth, milk, and hot sauce; simmer 2 minutes until thickened. Spoon some gravy over the fish-free chicken.
  6. Meanwhile, sear the shrimp in a second skillet with the remaining oil, 1–2 minutes per side, in batches. Slide them into the gravy to warm through.
  7. Stir the butter and cheddar into the grits. Bowl up the grits, top with shrimp and gravy (or the fish-free chicken), and scatter green onions.

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.5 from Budget Bytes' 1 lb shrimp / 1 cup grits / serves 4.
  • Kid heat: hot sauce cut from 1 Tbsp to 1 tsp in the gravy; use a mild Cajun/Creole blend (taste it first). Pass hot sauce.
  • Fish-free method change: Beth builds the gravy in the shrimp pan. Here the gravy goes in the chicken pan and the shrimp sears separately, so the fish-free portion never touches shrimp.
  • Old-fashioned grits are next to the oatmeal at Aldi/Sam's; they keep forever.
  • Not Grilled Lemon Garlic Shrimp or Shrimp Scampi Pasta — Southern, gravy over grits.
  • Mediterranean ✗ (butter, cheese, refined grits). moderate.
