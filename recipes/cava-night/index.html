Family DIY bowl-bar night, Cava-style. Kids build their own. Two proteins on the table so vegetarians and picky eaters can mix: harissa chicken and oven falafel. No boutique harissa jar — pantry spice mix.

Ingredients

Bases (put all three out)

  • 1 bag salad greens
  • Brown rice, ~4 cups cooked (house rule: always brown)
  • 6 pita, warmed

Proteins (both)

  • Harissa chicken — see Harissa Chicken Bowls (1.5 lb chicken, pantry spice mix)
  • Oven falafel — see Falafel Bowls (3 cans chickpeas)

Dips

  • 1 tub hummus (Aldi)
  • 1 tub tzatziki (Aldi) or 1 cup Greek yogurt + grated cucumber + garlic + lemon + salt
  • House harissa oil: 2 Tbsp paprika, 1 tsp cumin, pinch cayenne, 2 cloves garlic minced, 3 Tbsp olive oil, pinch salt — mix, serve in a small bowl. Adults drizzle; kids skip.

Toppings

  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
  • Quick pickled onion: 1 red onion, thinly sliced, covered with 1/2 cup vinegar + 1/2 cup hot water + 1 Tbsp sugar + 1 tsp salt. 20 minutes on the counter.
  • 4 oz crumbled feta
  • 1 bag coleslaw mix, tossed with 1 Tbsp olive oil + squeeze of lemon + pinch salt

Method

  1. Start the brown rice. Heat oven to 400°F for the falafel. Mix the pickled onion and the harissa oil.
  2. Make falafel and harissa chicken (methods on those cards). Warm the pita in the oven for the last 5 minutes.
  3. Set out bases, both proteins, dips, and toppings in bowls. Everyone builds.
  4. Kids: rice or pita, chicken or falafel, cucumber, hummus or tzatziki. Harissa oil and pickled onion on the side for whoever wants them.

Notes

  • Frozen falafel is fine if Aldi has it that week — skip the homemade batch. Don't make a special trip for a brand.
  • Not a clone of Greek Chicken Bowls (lemon-oregano chicken, no falafel, no harissa) or Tzatziki Bowls (taco-seasoned beef).
  • Mediterranean ✓: olive oil, poultry + legumes, vegetables, brown rice. light: lean proteins, dips on the side, no fryer.
  • Composed versions if you don't want the full bar: Harissa Chicken Bowls, Falafel Bowls, Chicken Shawarma Bowls, Roasted Veggie Hummus Bowls.
