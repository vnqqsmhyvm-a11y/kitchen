Turkey Minestrone

Italian vegetable soup with ground turkey, cannellini, small pasta, and kale. Turkey that isn't chili and isn't meatballs. Hits cooked tomato + legumes in one pot.

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1.5 lb ground turkey
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 2 celery ribs, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano (or 1 Tbsp fresh thyme)
  • 1 can (28 oz) diced tomatoes, with juices
  • 8 cups chicken broth
  • 2 cans (15 oz) cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup small pasta (shells, ditalini, or elbows)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • Pinch cayenne (optional — skip for kids)
  • 1 bag (5–8 oz) kale or baby spinach
  • Grated Parmesan and a drizzle of olive oil, to serve
  • Brown rice is not needed — the pasta is the grain. If you skip pasta, serve over brown rice (house rule: always brown).

Method

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium-high. Add the turkey and cook, breaking it up, until no pink remains. Push to the side.
  2. Add onion, carrots, celery, and garlic. Cook 6–8 minutes until the onion softens.
  3. Add oregano, tomatoes, broth, salt, and pepper. Bring to a boil.
  4. Stir in the pasta and beans. Simmer 10 minutes, until the pasta is tender. Don't add extra pasta — it swells.
  5. Stir in kale until wilted. Taste; add soy sauce for depth. Skip cayenne unless the adults want heat in their own bowls.
  6. Ladle into bowls. Parmesan and a little olive oil on top.

Notes

  • Feasting at Home writes this with leftover Thanksgiving turkey and homemade stock. Weeknight version: brown 1.5 lb ground turkey (Sam's/Aldi) and use boxed chicken broth. Same soup. Beans doubled to 2 cans so it actually feeds 6 as a dinner.
  • Pasta leftovers get mushy. If you know you'll freeze it, cook the pasta separately and add per bowl — or skip pasta and serve over brown rice.
  • Not a Turkey Chili clone (no taco seasoning / Rotel / corn) and not Turkey Meatballs in Tomato Sauce.
  • Mediterranean ✓: olive oil, poultry + legumes, vegetables, tomato.
