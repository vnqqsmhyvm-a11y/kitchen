Rajma (Kidney Bean Curry)

Swasthi's (Indian Healthy Recipes) rajma masala: kidney beans simmered in a thick onion-tomato gravy with ginger, garlic, coriander, cumin, and garam masala — North Indian "rajma chawal," the Punjabi equivalent of beans and rice. Canned-bean version makes it a 35-minute weeknight.

Ingredients

  • 3 cans (15 oz) dark red kidney beans, drained and rinsed (about 4 1/2 cups)
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil or vegetable oil (or ghee)
  • 1 bay leaf (optional)
  • 1 1/2 cups finely chopped onion (~1 1/2 onions)
  • 1 Tbsp minced garlic + 1 tsp minced ginger (jar is fine)
  • 2 1/4 cups tomato purée (one 15 oz can tomato sauce + 1 cup crushed tomatoes)
  • 3/4 tsp paprika + pinch cayenne (replaces Kashmiri chili powder; skip the cayenne for kids)
  • 1 tsp garam masala, plus 1/4 tsp to finish
  • 2 1/2 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 1/4 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/3 tsp turmeric
  • 3/4 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • 1 1/2 cups hot water
  • 3 Tbsp heavy cream (optional)
  • 2 Tbsp chopped cilantro; 1/2 lemon
  • To serve: brown basmati rice (house rule)

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium with the bay leaf. Add the onion and sauté until golden, 8–10 minutes.
  2. Stir in the garlic and ginger; cook 1 minute.
  3. Add the tomato purée and cook ~3–5 minutes until thick.
  4. Add the paprika, cayenne, garam masala, coriander, cumin, turmeric, and salt; sauté 2–3 minutes until the masala smells toasty and oil starts to separate at the edges.
  5. Add the beans and hot water. Simmer 10–15 minutes, until slightly thick. Mash a spoonful of beans against the pot to thicken it more.
  6. Taste; add salt and the extra 1/4 tsp garam masala. Stir in the cream if using, then cilantro and a squeeze of lemon.
  7. Serve over brown basmati rice.

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.5 from Swasthi's 2 cans / serves 4, using her canned-bean option (her dried-bean method needs an overnight soak + pressure cooker).
  • One-offs dropped: kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves) and Kashmiri chili powder are Indian-grocery items — paprika + a pinch of cayenne stands in for the color and mild heat; methi is optional in her recipe. Green chili omitted for the kids.
  • Ground cumin goes in with the other spices instead of whole cumin seeds.
  • Not Red Beans and Brown Rice — no sausage, Indian spices; not Chili — no meat, coriander/garam masala profile.
  • Mediterranean ✓: olive oil, legumes, tomato/onion, brown rice. light (moderate if you add the cream).
