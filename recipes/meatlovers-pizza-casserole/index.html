Meatlovers Pizza Casserole

Pasta casserole with ground beef, Italian sausage, pepperoni, pizza sauce, and a mozzarella–cheddar top. Pizza night in a 9×13. Not Turkey Baked Ziti (turkey, ricotta, marinara) and not Baked Ziti Casserole (beef + ricotta, no pepperoni).

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1/2 lb Italian sausage
  • 8 oz pepperoni slices
  • 3 cups pizza sauce
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 12 oz pasta (penne or rotini)
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning

Method

Source method (Amanda Flavours thread) — same bake for all six; only the mix and a couple of assembly notes change.

  1. Prep: Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Grease a 9×13-inch dish. Brown the ground beef and Italian sausage together; drain. Cook the pasta until slightly underdone; drain.
  2. Mix: In a bowl, combine the cooked meats, pasta, pizza sauce, Italian seasoning, half the mozzarella, and half the cheddar. Stir until evenly mixed. Fold in most of the pepperoni, saving some slices for the top.
  3. Assemble: Spread the mixture in the dish. Top with the remaining cheeses and the reserved pepperoni.
  4. Bake uncovered 25–30 minutes until cheese is melted and bubbly. Cover loosely with foil if the top browns too fast.
  5. Rest 5 minutes before serving so it sets.

Notes

  • Servings: source says serves 8 / bake 30 min. Household is 6 — keep the original amounts; leftovers cover lunches.
  • Red / processed meat: ground beef + Italian sausage + pepperoni. Counts toward the ≤2 red/processed-meat cap (processed meat is in the same bucket).
  • WW tier heavy: red-meat-centric + processed meat + 3 cups cheese + refined pasta. This is the week's one heavy when it appears.
  • Mediterranean ✗: red/processed meat, refined pasta, cheese-heavy (0–1 of 4).
  • Cooked tomato: pizza sauce counts toward the weekly cooked-tomato target.
  • a fish-free plate / hidden fish: no fish in the recipe. Check the Italian sausage and pizza sauce labels for anchovy/Worcestershire if a brand is unfamiliar.
