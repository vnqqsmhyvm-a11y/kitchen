Chicken Broccoli Rice Casserole

Creamy chicken, broccoli, and brown rice bake under cheddar and Parmesan. Not Chicken & Stuffing Casserole (stuffing top, no rice, no broccoli in the mix) — keep both cards; do not merge.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked chicken, shredded
  • 3 cups cooked brown rice (house rule: always brown rice — source just says "cooked rice")
  • 3 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 can (10.5 oz) cream of chicken soup
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder

Method

Source method (Amanda Flavours thread) — same bake for all six; only the mix and a couple of assembly notes change.

  1. Prep: Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Grease a 9×13-inch dish. Cook the brown rice until slightly underdone if starting from dry. Cook or shred the chicken if needed. Blanch or steam the broccoli florets 2–3 minutes until bright green (optional but keeps them tender-crisp).
  2. Mix: In a bowl, combine the chicken, brown rice, broccoli, cream of chicken soup, sour cream, garlic powder, half the cheddar, and half the Parmesan. Stir until evenly mixed.
  3. Assemble: Spread the mixture in the dish. Top with the remaining cheddar and Parmesan.
  4. Bake uncovered 25–30 minutes until cheese is melted and bubbly. Cover loosely with foil if the top browns too fast.
  5. Rest 5 minutes before serving so it sets.

Notes

  • Servings: source says serves 8 / bake 30 min. Household is 6 — keep the original amounts; leftovers cover lunches.
  • Brown rice: house rule (Diet Lens 2026-07-12) — always brown rice. Specify it on the card even though the source says only "cooked rice."
  • Not red meat: poultry. Does not use a red/processed-meat slot.
  • WW tier moderate: cream soup + sour cream + cheese, but poultry- and broccoli-forward with brown rice — classed like Chicken & Stuffing Casserole, not like Meatlovers Pizza Casserole.
  • Mediterranean ✓: poultry ✓, broccoli ✓, brown rice ✓; primary fat is cream soup not olive oil ✗ → 3 of 4.
  • a fish-free plate / hidden fish: no fish in the recipe. Check the cream of chicken soup label for hidden fish, Worcestershire, or anchovy; pick another brand (or a fish-free substitute) if listed.
  • Distinct from Chicken & Stuffing Casserole — different starch, different veg, different sauce build.
  • Nutrition: not given by the source; none listed here.
