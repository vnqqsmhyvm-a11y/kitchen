Shrimp and White Bean Stew

Allergy: Contains fish or shellfish — not for a fish-allergic eater; same-broth chicken and bean stew is in this card

Budget Bytes' shrimp and white bean stew: creamy butter beans simmered in lemony garlic-butter chicken broth with celery, onion, and grated parsnip, finished with tender shrimp, Parmesan, and lemon zest. Brothy, cozy, and a seafood night that doubles as a legume night.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 lb large peeled, deveined shrimp (thawed)
  • 1 parsnip (or carrot), peeled and grated
  • 3 celery stalks, thinly sliced
  • 3/4 yellow onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 lemon (zest; wedges to serve)
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 3 Tbsp salted butter
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 cans (15 oz) butter beans or cannellini, rinsed
  • 3 1/2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 1/4 tsp apple cider vinegar
  • 2 Tbsp grated Parmesan
  • Salt and pepper
  • To serve: crusty whole-grain bread or brown rice

Fish-free fallback (same broth and beans)

  • 1 small boneless chicken breast, cut into bite-size pieces (or 1 cup diced rotisserie chicken)

Method

  1. If using raw chicken for the fish-allergic eater, brown it in a small saucepan with a little oil, 5–6 minutes until 165°F. Set aside in that pan.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium. Cook the parsnip, celery, and onion until soft and glossy, not browned, ~6 minutes.
  3. Add the butter and garlic; cook 1 minute.
  4. Add the broth, vinegar, and beans. Simmer until reduced by about a quarter, 10–12 minutes.
  5. Ladle the fish-free portion (1 1/2 cups) into the fish-free saucepan with the chicken and keep warm. Add a pinch of Parmesan and zest to the fish-free portion.
  6. Add the raw shrimp to the big pot and simmer 2–3 minutes, stirring, until pink and opaque. Stir in the Parmesan and lemon zest; season.
  7. Serve with lemon wedges and bread or brown rice.

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.2 from Budget Bytes' 1 lb shrimp / 2 cans / serves 5; rounded up to 3 cans of beans to make it a full dinner for 6.
  • Fish-free method tweak: the original sautés the shrimp in the pot and then builds the broth on top of the drippings. Here the broth comes first, the fish-free share is pulled, and the shrimp poaches in the stew at the end (same 2–3 minute timing, just as tender).
  • Two-for-one: counts as a seafood dinner and a legume dinner. Shrimp isn't fatty fish.
  • Parsnip is optional — a carrot does the same job if Aldi doesn't have parsnips that week.
  • Not White Bean Kale Soup — shrimp, lemon-butter broth, no greens. Not Shrimp Scampi Pasta — beans instead of pasta.
  • Mediterranean ✓: olive oil, seafood, legumes, vegetables (butter is small). moderate (butter + Parmesan).
