Shrimp and Veggie Foil Packs

Allergy: Contains fish or shellfish — not for a fish-allergic eater; a same-seasoning chicken foil pack is in this card

Budget Bytes' foil packs: shrimp, zucchini, yellow squash, bell pepper, and red onion tossed with garlic, Italian seasoning, smoked paprika, and a splash of wine, sealed in foil with a pat of butter and grilled 8 minutes. No dishes, everyone gets their own pack.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 lb large shrimp, thawed, peeled, deveined
  • 2 1/2 cups zucchini, half-moons (~2 small)
  • 2 1/2 cups yellow squash, half-moons (~2 small)
  • 2 1/2 cups red bell pepper, sliced (~2)
  • 1 1/4 cups red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3/4 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 3/4 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 1/4 tsp salt + 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1/3 cup white wine or chicken broth
  • 2 1/2 Tbsp butter or olive oil, in 6 pats/drizzles
  • Lemon wedges
  • To serve: brown rice (house rule)

Fish-free pack (same seasoning, its own foil)

  • 1 small boneless chicken breast (~6 oz), sliced into thin 1/4-inch strips
  • Pinch each Italian seasoning, smoked paprika, salt; 1 tsp olive oil

Method

  1. Heat the grill to 400°F (or oven to 425°F).
  2. Make the fish-free pack first: toss the fish-free chicken strips with a handful of the vegetables, the pinch of seasonings, and oil. Seal in its own foil sheet and mark it (fold a corner or write "FF").
  3. In a large bowl, toss the shrimp, remaining vegetables, garlic, seasonings, and wine.
  4. Divide among 5 sheets of 12x18-inch heavy-duty foil, top each with a pat of butter, fold the long sides together twice, and crimp the ends up.
  5. The fish-free chicken pack goes on 8 minutes before the shrimp packs (grill 16–18 minutes total, or ~20 in the oven) — check that the chicken is 165°F.
  6. Add the shrimp packs and cook 8 minutes on the grill (12–14 in the oven). Rest 3 minutes, open carefully (steam), and serve with lemon and brown rice.

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.25 from Budget Bytes' 1 lb shrimp / 5 packs.
  • Fish-free swap: the fish-free chicken never shares foil with shrimp. Mix the fish-free pack before the shrimp bowl so there's no shared bowl or hands.
  • Oven works: a sheet pan of packs at 425°F — a good winter version.
  • Olive oil instead of butter keeps it squarely Mediterranean (seafood + vegetables + olive oil + brown rice = 4 of 4); with butter it's still 3 of 4. light.
  • Not Grilled Lemon Garlic Shrimp — steamed in foil with a full vegetable side built in, no brine or skewers.
