Tex-Mex Crockpot Chicken

Dump-and-go slow cooker chicken with black beans, corn, salsa, and cream cheese — shred and serve over brown rice or in tortillas. Kid-friendly Tex-Mex, minimal hands-on.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless skinless chicken breast (frozen or thawed)
  • 2 cans (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1.5–2 cups frozen corn (or 1 can, drained)
  • 1 jar (15–16 oz) salsa (chunky; medium or hot — the cream cheese mellows it)
  • 2 Tbsp taco seasoning
  • 1 package (8 oz) cream cheese, softened
  • Brown rice, cooked, to serve (house rule: always brown)
  • Optional to serve: shredded cheddar, sour cream or Greek yogurt, cilantro, green onion, pico de gallo, avocado

Method

  1. Place chicken in the crockpot with the black beans, corn, salsa, and taco seasoning.
  2. Cook on low 4–5 hours (or high 2.5–3 hours) until the chicken is cooked through and shreds easily.
  3. Remove the chicken, shred with two forks, and return it to the pot.
  4. Lay the softened cream cheese on top; cover and let sit ~30 minutes to melt.
  5. Stir until the cream cheese is fully combined and creamy. Serve over brown rice or in tortillas with any toppings.

Notes

  • Scaled from a 1.5 lb / serves-4 card to 2 lb chicken + doubled beans for a family of 6; leftovers reheat and freeze well (up to 3 months).
  • moderate tier: lean chicken + beans + corn is light, but a full 8 oz block of cream cheese is the defining fat — not heavy (no frying/red meat), but past light.
  • mediterranean: false — hits poultry/legumes-over-red-meat and brown-rice carbs, but cream cheese (not olive oil) is the primary fat and the vegetable content is thin (corn + salsa), landing it at 2 of 4. Flip to true if you'd rather count the beans/chicken more generously.
  • Salsa cooks down over hours but isn't a real cooked-tomato-sauce serving — the week's tomato quota is carried by Turkey Chili, not this.
  • Source: adapted from The Girl Who Ate Everything's Crockpot Fiesta Chicken, added 2026-08-02.
