Make-Ahead Bean and Cheese Burritos

Budget Bytes' freezer burritos: chili-cumin seasoned rice, black beans, cheddar, taco sauce, and green onion rolled in big tortillas — eat some tonight crisped in a skillet, freeze the rest for the busiest nights (microwave or oven from frozen).

Ingredients

Seasoned brown rice

  • 1 1/2 Tbsp oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 1/2 tsp chili powder
  • 1/3 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/3 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 1/2 cups long-grain brown rice
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • 3 1/4 cups water

Burritos

  • 3 cans (15 oz) black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 3 cups shredded cheddar
  • 3 green onions, sliced
  • 3/4 cup mild taco sauce
  • 12 large (10-inch) whole-wheat or regular flour tortillas
  • To serve: salsa, sour cream or Greek yogurt, a bagged salad

Method

  1. Rice: sauté the garlic in oil in a saucepan over medium for 1 minute. Add the chili powder, cumin, oregano, and rice; stir 2 minutes to toast.
  2. Add the water and salt, cover, and bring to a boil; simmer on low 40–45 minutes (brown rice). Rest 5–10 minutes covered, then fluff.
  3. Steam 4 tortillas at a time under a damp paper towel in the microwave for 30 seconds.
  4. Fill each with ~1/3 cup rice, 1/3 cup beans, 1 Tbsp taco sauce, 1/4 cup cheddar, and green onion. Fold in the sides and roll tightly.
  5. Tonight: crisp seam-side down in a skillet over medium-low until browned and the cheese melts, flipping once.
  6. Freezer: wrap each in parchment or plastic, bag, and freeze up to 3 months. Reheat from frozen: microwave on defrost ~9 minutes, or foil-wrapped at 350°F ~40 minutes.

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.5 from Budget Bytes' 8 burritos — 12 gives six dinner burritos plus six for the freezer (or seconds for the teens).
  • Brown rice (house rule): Beth uses white rice (1.5 cups water, 15 min). Brown rice takes ~3 1/4 cups water and 40–45 minutes — start it first, or use leftover brown rice warmed with the spices.
  • Mild taco sauce = kid heat. Sam's sells big packs of 10-inch tortillas.
  • Not Quesadillas / Black Bean Tacos — built for batch-and-freeze; the "make-ahead" lane was the thinnest in the deck.
  • Mediterranean ✗: legumes + brown rice = 2 of 4 (cheese-forward, no real vegetables, neutral oil). moderate for 3 cups cheddar and big tortillas.
