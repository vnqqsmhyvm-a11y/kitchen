Brazilian Rice

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 pound smoked sausage, sliced
  • 1/2 cup green onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup rice, uncooked
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 14 ounces pinto beans, drained and rinsed
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1–2 bay leaves

Method

  1. In a large skillet, heat oil over medium heat.
  2. Add sausage, green onion, and garlic and sauté until onions soften.
  3. Stir in rice, chicken broth, beans, salt and pepper, and bay leaves. Bring to a boil.
  4. Reduce to a low simmer and cover. Simmer 20–25 minutes until rice is done. Remove from heat and fluff with a fork.

Notes

  • Swap the oil for olive oil to nudge this toward the Mediterranean quota.
