Baked Tandoori Chicken

RecipeTin Eats' oven tandoori: drumsticks and thighs in a yogurt-spice marinade overnight, baked and basted, then blasted hot for charred spots. Mint yogurt and brown rice on the side. Marinate the night before; the day-of work is putting a tray in the oven.

Ingredients

Marinade

  • 1 3/4 cups plain yogurt (full fat or Greek)
  • 2 1/2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 1/2 tsp grated ginger (or 1 tsp ground)
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 2 Tbsp sweet paprika (stands in for Kashmiri chili powder — see Notes)
  • 1 1/4 tsp turmeric
  • 2 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 Tbsp ground coriander
  • Pinch cayenne, optional (skip for kids)
  • 1 1/4 tsp salt

Chicken

  • 3 lb bone-in chicken drumsticks and/or thighs (skin on or off)
  • Olive oil spray

Mint yogurt

  • 1 1/4 cups plain yogurt
  • 1/2 cup packed mint leaves (or 1 Tbsp dried mint)
  • Salt and pepper, drizzle of olive oil

To serve: brown rice (house rule: always brown), cucumber and tomato wedges

Method

  1. Mix the marinade in a large bowl. Add the chicken, coat, cover, and refrigerate 12–24 hours.
  2. Heat oven to 350°F. Line a sheet pan with foil and set a rack on it.
  3. Lift the chicken out, shaking off excess (keep the marinade), and lay it on the rack. Bake 20 minutes, then slather marinade on both sides.
  4. Bake 10 more minutes; dab more marinade on top. Bake 10 more; final baste, then spray with oil.
  5. Raise the oven to 450°F and bake 10–15 minutes until you get charred spots and the thickest pieces hit 175°F.
  6. Blitz or finely chop the mint into the yogurt with salt, pepper, and a little olive oil. Serve the chicken with brown rice, mint yogurt, and cucumber/tomato.

Notes

  • Scaled from RecipeTin's 2.4 lb chicken / serves 5 to 3 lb for 6 (marinade ×1.25).
  • Kashmiri chili powder is a one-off specialty buy — Nagi's own sub note allows paprika; we use sweet paprika for color and leave out the extra chili powder so kids can eat it. Add cayenne to an adults' portion of marinade if wanted.
  • Grill option (from the source): medium, lid closed, skin side down 5 minutes, baste, flip, ~20 minutes to 175°F. Good for a grill weekend.
  • Drumsticks are the cheap Sam's/Aldi family pack — same cut as Chicken Drumsticks Dry Rub, but a yogurt-masala marinade instead of a dry BBQ rub.
  • Mediterranean ✓: olive oil, poultry, brown rice (put cucumber/tomato on the plate for the vegetable). light.
