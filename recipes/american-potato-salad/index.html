American Potato Salad

Ingredients

  • 2 lb Yukon gold potato, peeled, cut into 1-inch chunks
  • 2 large eggs, hard-boiled
  • 2 tsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1/2 cup plain fat-free Greek yogurt
  • 1/4 cup light mayonnaise
  • 3 Tbsp sweet pickle relish
  • 1.5 tsp zero-calorie sweetener
  • 1/2 tsp table salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper, freshly ground
  • 1/2 cup celery, chopped/diced
  • 1/2 cup red onion, sliced/chopped

Method

  1. Boil potatoes in salted water until fork-tender, 7-9 minutes. Drain, cool slightly.
  2. Mash 1 egg yolk with vinegar until smooth. Add yogurt, mayo, relish, sweetener, salt, pepper; stir.
  3. Add potatoes, celery, onion; toss to coat. Chop remaining egg white + whole egg; fold in gently. Refrigerate until chilled.

Notes

  • Per serving (3/4 cup): 132 cal.
  • Lightened with Greek yogurt + light mayo instead of full mayo.
  • Plus chill time, not counted in total_time above.
