Chicken & Stuffing Casserole

Ingredients

  • 2 cans chicken
  • 2 cans cream of mushroom soup
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 1 1/2 cups water
  • 1 box Stovetop stuffing
  • 9x13 pan

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. Spread chicken in the pan.
  3. Mix soup and milk, pour over chicken.
  4. Melt butter, mix with water and stuffing, pour over chicken.
  5. Bake 30 minutes.

Notes

  • The ingredient list says water but step 3 says milk — confirm which (likely milk in the soup layer, water in the stuffing layer).
  • Assembles ahead well: build it the night before, bake the next day.
