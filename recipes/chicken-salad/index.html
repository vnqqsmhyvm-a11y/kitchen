House build, 2026-07-19 — trip-friendly sandwich filling.

Ingredients

  • 3 cans chicken (12.5 oz), drained and flaked
  • Mayonnaise to bind (~3/4 cup)
  • Garlic powder, to taste
  • Fresh-grated Parmesan (~1/2 cup)
  • Pecan halves (~3/4 cup)

Method

  1. Flake the chicken; fold in mayo, garlic powder, Parmesan, and pecans.
  2. Chill. Sandwich on bread — make the night before for trip lunches.

Notes

  • Debut: 2026-07-25 canoe trip lunch.
