Tuscan Salmon Skillet

Allergy: Contains fish or shellfish — not for a fish-allergic eater; same-sauce chicken is in this card

Budget Bytes' Tuscan salmon: seared salmon in a creamy Parmesan sauce with tomatoes, onion, spinach, and Italian herbs. Restaurant-style 30-minute fish night; the creamy sauce is how picky eaters get to salmon.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb salmon, skin removed, cut into 6 portions
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1/3 tsp salt + 1/4 tsp pepper (for the fish)
  • 2 1/2 Tbsp salted butter
  • 1 1/4 onions, diced
  • 2 tsp Italian herb blend
  • 1 1/4 tsp dried basil
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 4 Roma tomatoes, diced (seeded if you like)
  • 1 1/4 cups vegetable broth
  • 1/3 cup frozen spinach, squeezed dry
  • 1 cup heavy cream, room temperature
  • 1/3 tsp salt + 1/4 tsp pepper (for the sauce)
  • 2 1/2 oz Parmesan, grated (~1/3 cup)
  • 2 tsp minced parsley
  • To serve: brown rice (house rule: always brown), pasta, or crusty bread

Fish-free fallback (same sauce, own pan)

  • 1 boneless chicken breast, sliced into 2 thin cutlets

Method

  1. Rub the salmon with 1 Tbsp oil, salt, and pepper; let it sit while you prep.
  2. The fish-free chicken first: sear the fish-free seasoned cutlets in a small skillet with a little oil over medium-high, 3–4 minutes per side to 165°F. Keep them in that small skillet.
  3. In a large skillet, cook the remaining oil, butter, onion, Italian herbs, and basil over medium-high ~5 minutes until soft. Lower to medium; add garlic and tomatoes for 2 minutes.
  4. Add the broth, spinach, and cream; simmer 8–10 minutes. Stir in the salt, pepper, Parmesan, and parsley.
  5. Ladle about 1 cup of the finished sauce over the fish-free chicken in the fish-free skillet and keep it warm.
  6. Cook the salmon separately — easiest is a parchment-lined sheet pan at 400°F for 10–12 minutes (or sear it 2–3 minutes per side in another skillet) — then nestle it into the big skillet of sauce to warm through.
  7. Serve over brown rice or pasta.

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.25 from Budget Bytes' 1.2 lb salmon / serves 5.
  • Fish-free method tweak: Beth sears the salmon in the same skillet first and builds the sauce on its drippings. This card makes the sauce first, splits the fish-free share off, then cooks the salmon separately — so the fish-free sauce never touches fish. Roasting the salmon on a sheet pan is the easiest no-extra-skillet way.
  • Fatty fish ✓.
  • Not Honey Garlic Salmon / Teriyaki Salmon Bowls / Bang Bang Salmon — cream-tomato sauce, no glaze.
  • heavy: heavy cream + butter + Parmesan. Mediterranean ✗ (cream-based, even with olive oil and tomatoes). Count it as the week's one heavy night.
