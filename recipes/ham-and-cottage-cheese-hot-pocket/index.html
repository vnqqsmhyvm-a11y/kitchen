A quick pan-toasted breakfast pocket — tortilla, cottage cheese, cheddar, ham.

Ingredients

  • 2 small soft high-protein tortillas (~50 cal each)
  • 6 Tbsp cottage cheese (less-watery style)
  • 30g cheddar cheese, shredded
  • 2 slices deli ham
  • Olive oil spray

Method

  1. Lay a tortilla flat, spread cottage cheese, sprinkle cheddar, layer ham on half.
  2. Fold the tortilla over, press to seal.
  3. Spray a pan with olive oil, toast the pocket over medium heat 2-3 minutes per side until golden and the cheese melts.
  4. Repeat with the second tortilla, or make one larger folded pocket with both.
