Slow Cooker Chana Saag

Indian-inspired chickpea and spinach stew, slow-cooked, served over rice.

Ingredients

  • 30 oz canned reduced-sodium chickpeas, rinsed and drained
  • 14.5 oz canned diced tomatoes, drained
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tsp minced ginger
  • 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/4 cup vegetable broth (or water)
  • 5 oz baby spinach, chopped
  • 1 tsp fresh lemon juice
  • 2 cups cooked brown basmati rice, for serving
  • 2 Tbsp cilantro, chopped, for garnish (optional)

Method

  1. Combine chickpeas, tomatoes, onion, ginger, garlic, cumin, coriander, salt, turmeric, garam masala, and broth in the slow cooker. Cook on High 3 hours or Low 7-8 hours.
  2. Stir in spinach and lemon juice. Cover, cook 5-10 more minutes until spinach wilts.
  3. Serve over brown basmati rice, garnish with cilantro.

Notes

  • Per serving: 460 cal, 7g fat, 17g fiber, 20g protein (1 cup stew + 1/2 cup rice).
  • Card serves 4 — household is 6, so scale ~1.5x (45 oz chickpeas, etc.) for a real dinner.
  • Mediterranean ✓: legumes over red meat, real vegetables, brown rice — clears 3 of 4 despite the Indian cuisine.
