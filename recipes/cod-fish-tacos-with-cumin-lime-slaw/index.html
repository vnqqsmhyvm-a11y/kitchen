Cod Fish Tacos with Cumin-Lime Slaw

Budget Bytes' weeknight fish tacos: chili-cumin cod pan-seared in pieces, on toasted corn tortillas with a creamy cumin-lime coleslaw and avocado. Coleslaw mix makes the slaw a 2-minute job. Cod, not tilapia.

Ingredients

Cumin-lime slaw

  • 6 Tbsp mayonnaise (or half Greek yogurt)
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp lime juice (1–2 limes)
  • 3/4 tsp ground cumin
  • Pinch cayenne (optional)
  • 1/3 tsp salt
  • 3/4 tsp sugar
  • 3/4 of a 16 oz bag coleslaw mix
  • 3 green onions, sliced

Fish

  • 1 Tbsp chili powder
  • 3/4 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/3 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp oil, divided
  • 1.5 lb cod fillets (thawed if frozen), cut into taco-size pieces

Fish-free fallback (same spice, same method)

  • 1 boneless chicken breast, sliced into thin strips, + 1 tsp of the spice mix (set aside first)

To serve

  • 12–14 small corn tortillas
  • 1–2 avocados, sliced
  • Sliced jalapeño for adults; lime wedges

Method

  1. Toast the tortillas in a dry skillet over medium until browned on both sides. Stack and wrap in foil.
  2. Whisk the mayo, lime juice, cumin, cayenne, salt, and sugar. Toss with the coleslaw mix and green onions; refrigerate.
  3. Mix the chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, and salt. Set 1 tsp aside for the fish-free chicken. Season the fish pieces on both sides with the rest.
  4. Cook the fish-free chicken strips first: a little oil in the skillet over medium, 3–4 minutes per side until 165°F. Set aside on their own plate.
  5. Wipe the pan, add half the remaining oil, and cook half the fish a few minutes per side until opaque and flaking. Repeat.
  6. Build tacos: tortilla, fish (or chicken for the fish-allergic eater), avocado, slaw, jalapeño for whoever wants it.

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.5 from Budget Bytes' 1 lb cod / 8 tacos / serves 4.
  • Lean white fish — no omega-3. Doesn't count toward the fatty-fish quota; use it on a tilapia-swap week for variety (it's cod, so it isn't a fifth tilapia clone).
  • Fish-free swap: the fish-free chicken gets its own spice portion and cooks first, before any fish goes in the pan.
  • Not Blackened Tilapia tucked in a tortilla — different fish, milder chili-cumin rub, built as tacos with a dressed slaw.
  • Frozen cod from Sam's or Aldi is fine.
  • Mediterranean ✓ (just): fish, vegetables (slaw), corn tortillas count as whole grain — 3 of 4; neutral oil + mayo miss the olive-oil point. Use half Greek yogurt in the slaw to lean in. light.
