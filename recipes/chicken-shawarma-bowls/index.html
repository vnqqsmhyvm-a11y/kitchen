Chicken Shawarma Bowls

Yogurt-garlic-cinnamon shawarma chicken over brown rice with cucumber, tomato, red onion, and a dill yogurt sauce. Warm spices, not lemon-oregano — that's Greek Chicken Bowls.

Ingredients

Marinade

  • 2 lb boneless chicken breasts or thighs, cut into 1-inch strips
  • 3/4 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1.5 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3/4 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 3/4 tsp dried oregano
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1/4 tsp ground cloves
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil (for the skillet)

Yogurt sauce

  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 small garlic clove, minced
  • 1/2 tsp dried dill
  • Pinch salt

Bowls

  • Brown rice, ~4 cups cooked (house rule: always brown)
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced (or 1 pint cherry tomatoes)
  • 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced (ice-water soak 5 minutes if it's sharp)
  • Optional: pita, hummus, feta, greens

Method

  1. Mix marinade. Toss the chicken strips to coat. Fridge 4–24 hours. (If you forgot: 30 minutes is better than nothing, but it won't taste like shawarma.)
  2. Mix the yogurt sauce; fridge until dinner.
  3. Cook the brown rice. Soak the onion if using.
  4. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Shake excess marinade off the chicken. Cook in batches so it browns, 3–4 minutes per side, until 165°F. Rest 5 minutes.
  5. Build bowls: rice, chicken, cucumber, tomato, onion, yogurt sauce. Pita on the side for whoever wants it.

Notes

  • Budget Bytes marinade scaled from 1.25 lb chicken / serves 4 to 2 lb for 6. Their wrap becomes a bowl (they already list "bowl meal" as a serving). Brown rice, not the yellow rice house-rule miss and not the white rice on the old Greek-chicken card.
  • Cloves and nutmeg are pantry baking spices — don't buy a shawarma jar.
  • Not Greek Chicken Bowls (lemon + oregano only, no yogurt marinade, no cinnamon). Not Tzatziki Bowls (taco-seasoned ground beef). Not Yellow Rice and Chicken — brown rice always.
  • Mediterranean ✓: olive oil in the pan, poultry, vegetables, brown rice. light.
  • Full bar night: Cava Night.
