Lemon Chicken Orzo

One-skillet chicken over lemon-Parmesan orzo with spinach. Kid-mild — lemon, not cream sauce. Orzo is the pasta; no rice.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1 cup chopped yellow onion
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1.5 cups water
  • 1.5 cups orzo
  • 1/4 tsp salt, plus more to season the chicken
  • 1/8 tsp black pepper, plus more for the chicken
  • 1 Tbsp unsalted butter
  • 3/4 tsp lemon zest
  • 3 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 1/3 cup grated Parmesan
  • 2 cups chopped fresh spinach, loosely packed
  • Optional: parsley and lemon slices to serve

Method

  1. If the breasts are thick, pound to even thickness. Season with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat 1 Tbsp olive oil in a large heavy skillet over medium. Cook chicken until 165°F, 4–8 minutes per side. Move to a plate and tent with foil.
  3. Add remaining 1 Tbsp oil and the onion. Sauté 2–3 minutes until soft. Stir in garlic 30 seconds.
  4. Add broth, water, orzo, 1/4 tsp salt, and 1/8 tsp pepper. Bring to a simmer, then cover and keep at a low simmer 8–11 minutes until the orzo is tender. Stir now and then so it doesn't stick. Splash in water if it goes dry before the orzo is done.
  5. Stir in butter, lemon zest, lemon juice, Parmesan, and spinach until the spinach wilts. Slice the chicken against the grain and nestle it in. Parsley and lemon if you want.

Notes

  • Kristine's Kitchen already sizes this for 6 — no scale needed. Skip her optional 1/2 cup heavy cream; the orzo + Parmesan is creamy enough and keeps the tier at moderate.
  • Leftovers thicken; loosen with a splash of broth when reheating.
  • Mediterranean ✓: olive oil primary, poultry, spinach in the pan. Orzo is refined pasta, so it misses whole-ish carbs — 3 of 4 still clears.
  • Not a clone of Sheet-Pan Lemon Herb Chicken (that's oven chicken, no pasta).
