One-Pan Bang Bang Salmon Potato Bake

Baked salmon and potato with a bang bang (sweet-spicy yogurt) sauce.

Ingredients

  • 150g potato, cooked until soft
  • 4 oz salmon, cubed
  • Cooking spray
  • 1/4 tsp garlic salt
  • 1/4 tsp paprika
  • 1/8 tsp red chili flakes
  • 1/8 avocado, cubed
  • 1-2 Tbsp green onion, sliced
  • 1-2 Tbsp cilantro, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp sesame seeds

Bang Bang Yogurt Sauce

  • 1/4 cup Greek yogurt
  • 2 Tbsp sweet chili sauce
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 Tbsp water
  • 1 tsp sriracha
  • 1/8 tsp salt

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Cube the cooked potato and salmon.
  2. Spray a sheet pan, spread potato and salmon, season with garlic salt, paprika, and chili flakes.
  3. Bake 15-20 minutes until salmon is cooked through and potato edges crisp.
  4. Whisk the Bang Bang Yogurt Sauce ingredients together.
  5. Top with avocado, green onion, cilantro, sesame seeds, and a drizzle of the sauce.
