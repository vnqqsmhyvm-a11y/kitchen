Roasted Veggie Hummus Bowls

Roasted cauliflower, zucchini, and peppers over brown rice with a hummus swoop, tomatoes, and lemon. Vegetarian Cava bowl. Kids can build rice + hummus + the veg they like.

Ingredients

Roasted veg

  • 1 large head cauliflower, cut into florets (or 2 bags frozen cauliflower florets, thawed and dried)
  • 2 zucchini, cut into 1-inch chunks
  • 2 bell peppers, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 1 red onion, cut into wedges
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • Black pepper

Bowls

  • Brown rice, ~4 cups cooked (house rule: always brown)
  • 1 tub hummus (Aldi) — or 2 cans chickpeas blended with 1/4 cup olive oil, 2 cloves garlic, juice of 1 lemon, salt (Pinch of Yum, skip tahini)
  • 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
  • Lemon wedges
  • Optional: feta, pita, greens, parsley, leftover chicken for anyone who wants meat

Method

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. On two sheet pans (don't crowd), toss cauliflower, zucchini, peppers, and onion with olive oil, paprika, cumin, oregano, salt, and pepper. Roast 25–30 minutes until the cauliflower is browned at the edges.
  2. Cook the brown rice while the veg roasts.
  3. Swoop hummus into each bowl. Add rice, roasted veg, tomatoes. Lemon over the top. Feta and pita if you want.

Notes

  • Pinch of Yum is cauliflower + homemade hummus, plated as a swoop, serves fewer. Scaled to 6 with extra veg (zucchini + peppers — Aldi staples) and brown rice as the base so it's a dinner, not an appetizer plate. Budget Bytes Mediterranean hummus bowls (https://www.budgetbytes.com/mediterranean-hummus-bowls/) is the rice + hummus template; we dropped their ground beef so this stays the vegetarian composed card.
  • Store-bought hummus is the weeknight move. Homemade only if you already have extra chickpeas.
  • Not Sheet-Pan Chickpeas, Feta & Tomatoes: that one roasts chickpeas + feta as the protein. This is roasted mixed veg + hummus.
  • Not Greek Chicken Bowls — no chicken required (optional leftover chicken is a topping, not the point).
  • Mediterranean ✓: olive oil, legumes (hummus), vegetables, brown rice. light.
  • Full bar night: Cava Night.
