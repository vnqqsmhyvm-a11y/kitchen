Creamy Salmon Orzo with Peas

Allergy: Contains fish or shellfish — not for a fish-allergic eater; same-sauce chicken orzo is in this card

RecipeTin Eats' 15-minute one-pot orzo cooked risotto-style in milk and broth with peas and Parmesan, salmon folded in at the end. The fastest fatty-fish night in the deck — made with canned or leftover salmon instead of pricey hot-smoked.

Ingredients

  • 4 1/2 Tbsp unsalted butter
  • 3/4 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp flour
  • 3 cups milk
  • 3 1/3 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 2 1/4 cups dried orzo
  • 3 cups frozen peas
  • 3/4 cup grated Parmesan
  • 2 cans (5–6 oz) skinless boneless salmon, drained, or ~10 oz leftover cooked salmon, flaked (source: hot-smoked salmon — see Notes)
  • Salt and pepper; chopped parsley

Fish-free fallback (same sauce)

  • 1 cup shredded rotisserie chicken (or 1 can chicken, drained)

Method

  1. Melt the butter in a deep skillet or pot over medium-high. Cook the onion and garlic 2 minutes.
  2. Add the flour; stir 1 minute.
  3. Whisk in half the milk until smooth, then the rest of the milk, the broth, orzo, and peas.
  4. Bring to a simmer, then turn to medium so it bubbles gently. Cook 5–7 minutes, stirring often in the second half, until the orzo is just tender. It'll still look saucy — that's right.
  5. Off the heat, stir in Parmesan, salt, and pepper.
  6. Scoop the fish-free portion into a bowl now and stir the fish-free chicken into it.
  7. Gently fold the salmon into the pot in big flakes. Parsley on top; serve right away.

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.5 from RecipeTin's serves-4.
  • House swap: Nagi uses 6 oz hot-smoked salmon — great but a specialty price. Canned salmon (or the leftover half of a Baked Salmon in Foil night) gives the same fatty-fish credit for less. Bumped to ~10 oz so it's dinner-size.
  • Fatty fish ✓. No chili; very kid-friendly.
  • Fish-free swap: the fish-free bowl is pulled before the salmon goes in. Stir carefully so no flakes ride along on the spoon — use a clean spoon for the fish-free scoop.
  • Leftovers thicken a lot; loosen with milk.
  • Mediterranean ✗: butter + milk + refined orzo. moderate.
