Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas

Kristine's oven fajitas: sliced chicken, three bell peppers, and onion tossed in a homemade fajita spice and roasted hot on one pan. Taco-night energy without the ground beef, no packet seasoning, one pan to wash.

Ingredients

Fajita seasoning

  • 1 Tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar, optional
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne, optional (skip for kids)

Pan

  • 2 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts, sliced against the grain into 1/2-inch strips
  • 4 bell peppers (mixed colors), thinly sliced
  • 1 large yellow onion, thinly sliced
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of 1/2 lime
  • 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
  • 12–16 small flour (or corn) tortillas, warmed
  • Toppings: guacamole/avocado, sour cream or Greek yogurt, salsa, cheese

Method

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Mix the seasoning.
  2. Line a rimmed sheet pan (two if the pan is crowded) with parchment. Add chicken, peppers, and onion.
  3. Drizzle with olive oil, sprinkle with seasoning, toss to coat, and spread in an even layer.
  4. Bake 18–20 minutes total, tossing after 10, until the chicken is cooked through.
  5. Squeeze the lime over, scatter cilantro, and serve in warm tortillas with toppings.

Notes

  • Source serves 6 with 1.5 lb chicken / 3 peppers. Bumped to 2 lb / 4 peppers — the Chicken Kabobs feedback (ran short on 1.5 lb) applies here too. Use two pans so it roasts instead of steams.
  • Kid build: plain chicken + cheese in a tortilla works for anyone avoiding peppers.
  • Not Tacos or Quesadillas: sliced roasted chicken and peppers, no ground meat, no griddle.
  • Mediterranean ✓ on 3 of 4 (olive oil, poultry, vegetables); tortillas are refined. light with light toppings.
