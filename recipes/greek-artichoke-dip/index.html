Greek Artichoke Dip

Ingredients

  • 15 oz canned artichoke hearts, rinsed, drained, coarsely chopped
  • 22 Tbsp plain fat-free Greek yogurt
  • 2 Tbsp dill, chopped
  • 1 tsp jarred minced garlic
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/8 tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Coarsely chop artichokes, place in a mixing bowl.
  2. Stir in remaining ingredients.

Notes

  • Per serving (1/4 cup): 41 cal.
  • Despite the name, doesn't clear mediterranean: true — no olive oil, no carb component; only "vegetables present" (artichokes) clearly hits.
  • Tip: blot canned artichokes dry with paper towels before chopping.
