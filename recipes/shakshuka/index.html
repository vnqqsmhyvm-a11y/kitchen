Shakshuka

Budget Bytes' shakshuka: onion, red bell pepper, and celery cooked down in olive oil with smoked paprika, cumin, oregano, and cinnamon, simmered into a thick tomato-lemon stew, then eggs baked right in it. Served with toasted pita for scooping. Cheap, fast, and the deck's first egg dinner.

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1 1/2 large yellow onions, diced
  • 2 red bell peppers, diced (one replaces the jalapeños — see Notes)
  • 7 celery ribs, diced
  • 2 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3 Tbsp smoked paprika
  • 1/3 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 3/4 tsp black pepper
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 can (28 oz) fire-roasted or regular diced tomatoes, with juices
  • 3 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 1/2 lemons: zest + 1 1/2 Tbsp juice
  • 1 Tbsp sugar
  • 1–1 1/2 cups water, as needed
  • 10–12 eggs (about 2 per adult/teen, 1 per kid)
  • 3 Tbsp chopped parsley
  • To serve: whole-wheat pita, toasted; sliced avocado; crumbled feta (optional)

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
  2. In your largest oven-safe skillet (12-inch+), heat the olive oil over medium. Add the onion, bell peppers, celery, salt, smoked paprika, cinnamon, oregano, cumin, and black pepper. Cook 10–12 minutes until soft and fragrant.
  3. Add the garlic, tomatoes, tomato paste, lemon zest and juice, and sugar; cook 10 minutes. Add water as needed for a thick stew consistency (like chili, not paste).
  4. Make 10–12 dents with the back of a spoon and crack an egg into each. (If it won't all fit, split the sauce between two skillets.)
  5. Bake 8–12 minutes until the whites are set and yolks are still soft (or longer for firm yolks for the kids).
  6. Top with pepper and parsley. Serve with toasted pita and avocado.

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.5 from Budget Bytes' sauce for 4; eggs bumped from "3, optional" to 10–12 so it's a full dinner for 6.
  • Kid heat / one-offs: jalapeños and cayenne dropped (a second red pepper replaces them); 1 1/2 tsp cardamom dropped — it's a pricey specialty jar that would only be used here. Hot sauce or harissa on the side for adults.
  • First egg-protein dinner in the deck. Breakfast-for-dinner alternative to Breakfast Burritos.
  • Mediterranean ✓: olive oil, vegetables, cooked tomato, whole-wheat pita; eggs stand in for meat. light.
