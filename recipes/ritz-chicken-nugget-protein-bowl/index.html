Baked Ritz-crusted chicken nuggets over a cottage cheese and avocado bowl.

Ingredients

  • 5 oz raw chicken breast, cubed
  • 50-60g Ritz crackers, crushed
  • 1 whole egg, beaten
  • Spray oil
  • Salt, to taste
  • Chili flakes, to taste
  • 1/2 cup low-fat cottage cheese
  • 1/4 avocado
  • 2 tsp honey
  • Salt, to taste (bowl)
  • Chili flakes, to taste (bowl)

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Crush the Ritz crackers.
  2. Dip chicken cubes in beaten egg, then coat in crushed crackers.
  3. Spray a baking sheet, arrange nuggets, spray tops lightly, season with salt and chili flakes.
  4. Bake 15-18 minutes until golden and cooked through (165°F internal).
  5. Build the bowl: cottage cheese, honey, salt, chili flakes, top with the nuggets and avocado.
