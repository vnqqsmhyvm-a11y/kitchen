Turkey Zucchini Boats

Budget Bytes' zucchini boats — halved, pre-roasted zucchini filled with a marinara-meat filling (plus the scooped-out zucchini) and baked under mozzarella. Made with ground turkey instead of pork sausage to keep it out of the red/processed cap. Low-carb pizza energy.

Ingredients

  • 6 large zucchini (about 3 lb)
  • 2 1/2 Tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1.5 lb ground turkey (or turkey Italian sausage)
  • 1 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning (1 1/2 tsp total — source's 1/2 tsp plus extra because turkey isn't pre-seasoned)
  • 1/2 tsp fennel seed, optional (gives turkey a sausage flavor)
  • 1 1/2 small yellow onions, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 1/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 3/4 tsp black pepper, divided
  • 1/3 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 1/4 cups marinara sauce
  • 1/3 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • Chopped parsley, optional

Method

  1. Heat oven to 375°F. Halve the zucchini lengthwise and scoop out the centers, leaving a 1/4–1/2 inch rim. Chop the scooped flesh and set it aside.
  2. Put the boats on parchment-lined sheet pans. Brush the insides with ~1 Tbsp oil and season with some of the salt and pepper. Pre-bake 15 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, heat the remaining oil in a large skillet over medium. Brown the turkey with the Italian seasoning and fennel.
  4. Add onion and garlic; cook until the onion is translucent.
  5. Add the rest of the salt and pepper, garlic powder, marinara, breadcrumbs, and half of the chopped zucchini flesh. Simmer 5 minutes.
  6. Fill the boats, top with mozzarella, and bake 15 minutes until melty and bubbling. Parsley on top.

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.5 from Budget Bytes' 4 zucchini / 1 lb sausage (serves 4, two boats each) to 6 zucchini / 1.5 lb for 6.
  • Protein swap: the source uses Italian pork sausage. We use ground turkey + extra Italian seasoning (+ optional fennel) so it's a poultry night, not processed meat. Use turkey Italian sausage if Aldi has it.
  • Use the leftover zucchini flesh in the next pasta sauce or soup.
  • Serve with a slice of garlic bread or a side salad for the bigger eaters.
  • Mediterranean ✓ on 3 of 4 (olive oil, poultry, vegetables + tomato). moderate for the mozzarella.
