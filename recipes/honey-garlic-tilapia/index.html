Honey Garlic Tilapia

Allergy: Contains fish or shellfish — not for a fish-allergic eater; plan a simple fish-free fallback

The tilapia twin of Honey Garlic Salmon — same honey-garlic glaze, leaner white fish. Quick pan dinner over brown rice for a tilapia-swap week.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb tilapia fillets
  • 3 Tbsp honey
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 Tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper
  • ~3 cups pan vegetables (broccoli florets, bell pepper, or green beans)
  • Brown rice, cooked, to serve

Method

  1. Whisk together the honey, garlic, soy sauce, and lemon juice.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium-high. Pat the tilapia dry, season, and sear 3–4 minutes per side until flaky. Remove.
  3. Add the vegetables to the pan and sauté a few minutes; pour in the glaze and simmer 1–2 minutes to thicken.
  4. Return the fish to the pan and spoon the glaze over. Serve over brown rice.

Notes

  • Fish-free swap: same-glaze chicken breast from the bag — the proven fallback from the salmon version.
  • Lean white fish — no omega-3. Does NOT count toward the fatty-fish quota (tilapia-swap week per Diet Lens).
  • mediterranean: true — olive oil, fish over red meat, real vegetables, brown rice.
