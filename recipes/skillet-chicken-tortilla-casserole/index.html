Skillet-on-the-Grill Chicken Tortilla Casserole

Layered casserole cooked in a cast-iron skillet, on the grill or in the oven.

Ingredients

  • 4 sprays cooking spray
  • 1.5 cups uncooked corn
  • 1.5 cups canned low-sodium black beans
  • 16 oz chicken, cooked and shredded (doubled from the card's 8 oz — not enough for 6 people; on-hand frozen chicken breasts cover it)
  • 9 medium corn tortillas, cut in half
  • 2 cups salsa
  • 3/4 cup shredded reduced-fat Mexican-style cheese
  • 1/3 cup scallions, thinly sliced
  • 1 medium Fresno chile pepper, thinly sliced (jalapeño works if Fresno isn't available)

Method

  1. Coat a 10-inch cast-iron skillet with cooking spray. Heat on a grill (lid closed, medium-high ~425°F) or in an oven preheated to 425°F.
  2. Combine corn, black beans, and chicken in a bowl. Layer: 6 tortilla halves in the pan, half the chicken mixture, 3/4 cup salsa; repeat; top with remaining 6 tortilla halves and 1/2 cup salsa. Sprinkle cheese. Cover with foil (sprayed side down). Cook 15 minutes (grill lid closed, or in the oven).
  3. Remove foil, top with scallions and chile. Cook uncovered 3-4 minutes until cheese is melted and edges crisp. Cut into 6 wedges.

Notes

  • Card's per-serving nutrition (294 cal) reflects the original 8 oz chicken — no longer accurate after doubling; not worth guessing a new number without recalculating.
  • Light tier: lean chicken, corn+beans vegetable-forward, minimal cheese, holds even with the extra protein.
  • Overlaps ingredients with Chicken & Rice Bowls (chicken, beans, salsa) — don't schedule both the same week.
