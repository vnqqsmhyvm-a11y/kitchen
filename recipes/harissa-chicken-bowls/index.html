Harissa Chicken Bowls

Composed Cava-style bowl: smoky paprika-cumin chicken, brown rice, cucumber, tomato, hummus, feta. No harissa paste jar — pantry spice mix does the same job.

Ingredients

House harissa (pantry mix — no boutique jar)

  • 2 Tbsp paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • Pinch cayenne (skip for kids; adults can add more at the table)
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice

Bowls

  • 2 lb boneless chicken breasts or thighs, cut into 2-inch chunks
  • Brown rice, ~4 cups cooked (house rule: always brown)
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 tub hummus
  • 4 oz crumbled feta
  • Optional: pita, greens, pickled onion (see Cava Night)

Method

  1. Mix the house harissa in a bowl. Toss the chicken to coat. 20 minutes on the counter is enough; up to overnight in the fridge is better.
  2. Cook the brown rice.
  3. Heat a large skillet over medium (no extra oil — the marinade has it). Cook the chicken, stirring, until browned and 165°F, 8–10 minutes. Don't crowd; two batches if needed.
  4. Build bowls: rice, chicken, cucumber, tomato, a scoop of hummus, feta. Kids can skip feta and keep the chicken mild (cayenne was only a pinch).

Notes

  • Not Greek Chicken Bowls: those are lemon-oregano, no cumin/paprika heat. Not Chicken & Rice Bowls: those are Tex-Mex (black beans, salsa, avocado).
  • Mediterranean ✓: olive oil, poultry, vegetables, brown rice. light.
  • Full bar night: Cava Night.
