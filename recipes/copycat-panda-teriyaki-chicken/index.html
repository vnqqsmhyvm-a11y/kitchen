The link from your old Dining Out note — a kid-proven comfort dish. When the family wants takeout vibes on a home-cooked budget.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb chicken thighs or breasts
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch + 2 tablespoons water (slurry)
  • Serve with steamed rice and broccoli

Method

  1. Mix soy sauce, honey, brown sugar, garlic, and ginger.
  2. Cook chicken in a skillet over medium-high heat until done.
  3. Pour sauce over the chicken, bring to a simmer.
  4. Stir in cornstarch slurry and cook 1–2 minutes until sauce thickens.
  5. Serve over rice.
