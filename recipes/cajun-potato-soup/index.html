Cajun Potato Soup

Creamy Cajun potato soup with smoked sausage and potatoes. One-pot stovetop dinner — not Sausage & Fried Potatoes (fried skillet, no broth) and not Jambalaya (rice, chicken).

Ingredients

  • 1 lb smoked sausage, sliced
  • 5 medium potatoes, peeled and diced
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 tablespoon flour
  • 1 tablespoon Cajun seasoning
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Chopped parsley for garnish

Method

  1. In a large pot, brown the sliced sausage over medium heat. Remove and set aside.
  2. Melt the butter in the same pot. Add the onion, bell pepper and garlic, and cook until softened.
  3. Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Add the potatoes, Cajun seasoning, smoked paprika, salt, pepper and chicken broth.
  5. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer 15–20 minutes until the potatoes are tender.
  6. Lightly mash a few potatoes to make it creamier.
  7. Stir in the cream, cheddar and cooked sausage.
  8. Simmer 5 more minutes until thick and heated through.
  9. Garnish with parsley and extra black pepper.

Notes

  • Source (Flavorful Kitchen Adventures on X) gives no serving count; amounts kept as written and treated as ~6 for this household.
  • Kid heat: Cajun seasoning brands vary a lot in salt and heat — start with less (or half) for the kids and finish at the table with more seasoning or hot sauce.
  • Smoked sausage + potatoes + cream/cheese staples: Sam's or Aldi.
  • One-pot, about 40 minutes total — good weeknight slot.
  • Optional lighter swap (not the default recipe): replace some or all of the heavy cream with half-and-half or milk; still cheese-forward, so it may stay heavy or land at the top of moderate.
  • Smoked sausage is processed meat — counts toward the ≤2 red/processed-meat cap.
  • Mediterranean ✗: butter/cream/cheese as the fat base, processed sausage as the protein (vegetables + potatoes alone don't clear 3 of 4). heavy (cream-based + cheese).
