No-Added-Sugar Blueberry Muffins

Ingredients

  • 1 small, very ripe banana
  • 1/2 cup unsweetened applesauce
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 Tbsp canola oil
  • 2 cups self-rising flour
  • 1.5 cups blueberries

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Line a 12-cup muffin pan.
  2. Process banana and applesauce until smooth. Add eggs and oil, pulse to combine.
  3. Combine flour and banana mixture in a bowl. Fold in blueberries. Divide among muffin cups. Bake ~15 minutes until a tester comes out clean.

Notes

  • Per serving (1 muffin): 129 cal, 4g sugar (from the fruit only — no added sugar).
