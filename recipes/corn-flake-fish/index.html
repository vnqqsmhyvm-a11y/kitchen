Corn Flake Fish

Allergy: Contains fish or shellfish — not for a fish-allergic eater; plan a simple fish-free fallback

Born 2026-07-19 — a house idea: the Corn Flake Chicken coating on the freezer's bass/crappie/sunfish stash.

Ingredients

  • ~2 lb bass / crappie / sunfish fillets, thawed and patted dry
  • 3 cups crushed corn flakes
  • 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1-1/2 tsp seasoned salt
  • 3 large eggs, beaten
  • 1/4 cup butter, melted
  • Fish-free fallback: 2 chicken tenders through the same breading stations

Method

  1. Preheat 375° (thin fillets run hotter and faster than chicken). Butter a sheet pan.
  2. Flour + seasoned salt + corn flakes in one shallow dish, beaten eggs in another.
  3. Dip fillets: egg → crumbs, press to coat. Onto the pan; drizzle remaining butter.
  4. Bake ~12-15 min until the fish flakes at the thickest point.
  5. The fish-free chicken tenders: same coating, on the same pan ~5 min ahead of the fish, to 165°.

Notes

  • Convection: check 2 min early (teriyaki salmon lesson, 2026-07-19).
  • Fish night — never counts toward the all-family-favorites quota; a fish-free plate always gets the fallback.
