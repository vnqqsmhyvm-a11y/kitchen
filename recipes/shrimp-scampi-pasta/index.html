Shrimp Scampi Pasta

Allergy: Contains fish or shellfish — not for a fish-allergic eater; same-sauce chicken is in this card

Garlic-lemon shrimp over linguine. Kid-mild — skip the red-pepper flakes in the family pan. Shrimp is shellfish, not fatty fish — does not count toward the salmon quota. Still counts as a fish night (fish-free fallback below).

Ingredients

  • 1 lb linguine or spaghetti
  • 2 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined (frozen is fine — thaw in the fridge)
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 2 Tbsp unsalted butter
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced or slivered
  • Pinch red-pepper flakes (adult bowls only)
  • 1/2 cup dry white wine if already open, or chicken broth
  • 2 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 Tbsp chopped parsley (optional)
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Vegetable side: steamed broccoli or a bag of salad

Fish-free fallback (same sauce, same night)

  • 2 boneless chicken breasts (~1 lb), cut into 1-inch pieces
  • Extra garlic, lemon, olive oil — don't share the shrimp pan

Method

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water until al dente. Drain, saving 1/2 cup pasta water.
  2. Pat shrimp dry. Season lightly with salt.
  3. Fish-free plate first (separate skillet): heat 1 Tbsp olive oil over medium. Cook the fish-free chicken pieces until 165°F, about 6–8 minutes. In the last minute, add a spoonful of garlic, a squeeze of lemon, salt, and pepper. Keep warm. Never use the shrimp pan for the fish-free food.
  4. Family skillet: heat remaining 2 Tbsp olive oil and the butter over medium-high. Add garlic (and a pinch of flakes only if the adults want heat in their own bowls). Cook ~1 minute until fragrant, not brown.
  5. Add shrimp in an even layer. Cook until just pink, about 2 minutes. Add wine or broth; boil 1–2 minutes. Flip shrimp; cook 1 minute more until opaque. Off heat: lemon juice, parsley, pepper.
  6. Toss pasta into the shrimp skillet with a splash of pasta water. Serve with the vegetable. The fish-free plate gets the fish-free chicken over a separate portion of plain pasta, same lemon-garlic idea, never tossed with the shrimp.

Notes

  • Simply Recipes scampi is 1 lb shrimp, no pasta quantities. Scaled to 2 lb shrimp + 1 lb pasta for 6. Butter kept to 2 Tbsp so olive oil stays the primary fat.
  • Wine only if a bottle is already open — otherwise broth. Don't buy a bottle for this.
  • Fish-free swap: same-sauce chicken in a separate skillet, same night. Quesadillas only if you're not already at the stove. Never toss the fish-free pasta in the shrimp pan.
  • Shrimp does not count toward the fatty-fish / omega-3 quota. Still triggers the fish-night fallback.
  • Mediterranean ✓: olive oil, seafood over red meat, vegetable on the plate. Pasta is refined — 3 of 4.
  • Not a clone of Grilled Lemon Garlic Shrimp (that's grill + brown rice, no pasta).
