Spinach Lasagna Roll-Ups

Budget Bytes' lasagna roll-ups: lasagna noodles spread with a spinach-ricotta-mozzarella-Parmesan filling, rolled, covered in a simple olive-oil marinara, topped with mozzarella, and baked. Lasagna flavor without layering, and the kids each get "their own" roll.

Ingredients

Filling and noodles

  • 1 lb lasagna noodles (~18 noodles; a few spare for tears)
  • 15 oz whole-milk or part-skim ricotta
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/4 tsp salt + 1/4 tsp pepper
  • 10 oz frozen chopped spinach, thawed and squeezed very dry
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella (topping)

Marinara (or a 24–28 oz jar)

  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small yellow onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1/2 tsp dried basil + 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp brown sugar (optional) + 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F.
  2. Sauce (if making): cook onion and garlic in olive oil over medium 5 minutes. Stir in the tomato paste until darkened, 3–5 minutes. Add crushed tomatoes, herbs, sugar, and salt; simmer partially covered 30 minutes.
  3. Boil the noodles in salted water just to al dente (too soft and they tear). Drain.
  4. Mix the spinach, ricotta, 1 cup mozzarella, Parmesan, egg, salt, and pepper.
  5. Lay out noodles; spread ~1/4 cup filling edge to edge on each and roll up. Set seam-side down in a 9x13 dish (about 16 rolls).
  6. Pour the sauce over and top with the remaining 1 cup mozzarella.
  7. Cover with foil and bake 30 minutes. Serve hot.

Notes

  • Budget Bytes' recipe is 8 servings of 2 rolls — kept as written; six of us eat 2–3 each, and leftovers reheat well. No scaling needed.
  • Jarred marinara is fine on a busy night; the homemade sauce is what earns the olive-oil point.
  • Not Turkey Baked Ziti / Spinach Ricotta Chicken Bake — meatless, rolled. Counts as a cooked-tomato-sauce night.
  • Mediterranean ✓ (just): olive oil (sauce), vegetarian, spinach + tomato; refined noodles miss — 3 of 4. moderate for the cheese.
