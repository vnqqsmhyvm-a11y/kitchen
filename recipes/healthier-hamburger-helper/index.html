Healthier Hamburger Helper

One-pot beef and pasta skillet with zucchini, spinach, and cheddar — a lightened take on the box version.

Ingredients

  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lb extra lean ground beef
  • 1 small white onion, diced small
  • 1 medium zucchini, diced small
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 Tbsp chili powder
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • Black pepper, to taste, more for serving
  • 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper flakes, optional, more for serving
  • 1 13-oz box uncooked whole wheat or protein pasta (macaroni or orecchiette)
  • 2.5 cups beef broth
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 big handfuls baby spinach, roughly chopped
  • 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese

Method

  1. Heat oil in a large non-stick skillet or pot over medium heat.
  2. Add beef, onion, and zucchini. Break up the meat with a spatula until in small, even crumbles. Cook until the meat has minimal pink remaining and vegetables are slightly softened, about 6-8 minutes.
  3. Add garlic, tomato paste, chili powder, salt, pepper, and crushed red pepper. Cook 1-2 minutes until garlic is just fragrant, stirring frequently.
  4. Add pasta, broth, and milk. Stir, making sure everything is submerged under liquid — add more broth as needed.
  5. Cook uncovered 8-12 minutes until pasta is al dente, stirring occasionally, at a gentle simmer (turn down if it's at a rolling boil).
  6. Stir in baby spinach and cheese until melted and combined. If there's excess liquid, rest 5 minutes and stir again. Serve warm with black pepper and grated cheddar.
