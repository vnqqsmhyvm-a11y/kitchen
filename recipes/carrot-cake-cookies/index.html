Carrot Cake Cookies

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup dried dates, pitted
  • 1.25 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1.75 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp ground ginger
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/8 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 5 Tbsp unsalted butter, room temperature
  • 2 Tbsp sugar
  • 1 Tbsp 1% low-fat milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 1.5 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 medium carrots, finely grated then finely chopped

Method

  1. Cover dates in boiling water, soak 4-5 min, drain. Process to a smooth purée.
  2. Whisk flour, baking soda, cinnamon, ginger, salt, nutmeg. Beat butter and sugar until fluffy; add date purée, milk, egg, vanilla. Fold in flour mixture, then carrots. Chill dough 3 hours or overnight.
  3. Preheat oven to 350°F. Scoop 24 balls (1 heaping Tbsp each), roll smooth, flatten with a criss-cross fork pattern, spaced 2 inches apart on parchment-lined sheets.
  4. Bake ~10 minutes until lightly browned. Cool 10 min on the sheet, then a wire rack.

Notes

  • Per serving (1 cookie): 69 cal.
  • Most of the 3hr20min "prep" is inactive chill time, not hands-on.
