Amanda Flavours Tuna Noodle Casserole

Cream-of-mushroom tuna noodle bake with peas, cheddar, and a buttery cracker top. This is a fish dish. Not the from-scratch Tuna Noodle Casserole (Budget Bytes — butter-roux, cream cheese, no canned soup); keep both cards; do not overwrite or merge.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz egg noodles
  • 2 (5 oz) cans tuna, drained
  • 1 can (10.5 oz) cream of mushroom soup
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 1/2 cup diced onions
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup crushed buttery crackers

Fish-free fallback (same sauce — cook first)

  • ~1 cup cooked chicken, shredded (rotisserie or pan-cooked breast/thigh)
  • Small loaf pan or 1-qt baking dish
  • A share of the cream-soup sauce, noodles, peas, onion, cheese, and cracker top — assembled before the tuna goes into the family mix

Method

Source method (Amanda Flavours thread) — same bake for all six; only the mix and a couple of assembly notes change.

  1. Prep: Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Grease a 9×13-inch dish (and the fish-free small pan). Cook the egg noodles until slightly underdone; drain. Dice the onion; cook briefly in a skillet until soft if you want it milder.
  2. Sauce base (shared): In a bowl, whisk the cream of mushroom soup with the milk. Stir in the peas, onion, half the cheddar, and the drained noodles until evenly mixed.
  3. Fish-free plate first: Scoop a portion of the noodle–sauce mix into the fish-free small pan with the cooked chicken (no tuna). Top with a little cheddar and crushed crackers.
  4. Family mix: Fold the drained tuna into the remaining mixture. Spread in the 9×13. Top with the remaining cheddar and the crushed crackers.
  5. Bake uncovered 25–30 minutes until cheese is melted and bubbly and the cracker top is golden. Cover loosely with foil if the top browns too fast.
  6. Rest 5 minutes before serving so it sets.

Notes

  • Servings: source says serves 8 / bake 30 min. Household is 6 — keep the original amounts; leftovers cover lunches.
  • FISH DISH — a fish-free plate: allergic to fish. Same creamy sauce, chicken instead of tuna, the fish-free pan assembled and preferably baked first (or beside the family dish). Never serve tuna to that plate.
  • Hidden fish: check the cream of mushroom soup label for Worcestershire/anchovy/fish. If listed, replace with a fish-free cream soup (or a quick milk + sautéed mushroom + flour sauce).
  • Fatty fish? Canned tuna is lean — does not reliably count as the weekly fatty-fish quota (salmon/sardines do). Still counts as a fish night for fish-allergy protocol purposes.
  • WW tier moderate: cream soup + cheese + crackers, but simpler than the from-scratch Budget Bytes version marked heavy (Tuna Noodle Casserole). Condensed-soup style, not a butter-roux + cream-cheese bomb.
  • Mediterranean ✗: cream-soup fat ✗, refined egg noodles ✗; fish ✓ and peas/onion ✓ (2 of 4).
  • Distinct from Tuna Noodle Casserole (Budget Bytes) and Mediterranean Tuna Pasta.
  • Nutrition: not given by the source; none listed here.
