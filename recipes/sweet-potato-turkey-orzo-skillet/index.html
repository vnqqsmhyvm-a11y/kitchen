Sweet Potato & Ground Turkey Orzo Skillet

Budget Bytes' one-pan fall skillet: browned ground turkey, sweet potato cubes, sage-thyme-smoked paprika, orzo simmered in broth, kale wilted in, Parmesan stirred through. Thanksgiving flavors on a Tuesday.

Ingredients

  • 3 Tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1.5 lb ground turkey
  • 1 1/2 yellow onions, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 1/2 medium sweet potatoes, peeled, 1/2-inch cubes (about 2 1/4 cups)
  • 3/4 tsp dried thyme
  • 3/4 tsp rubbed sage
  • 3/4 tsp poultry seasoning
  • 3/4 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/3 tsp black pepper, divided
  • 1 1/2 cups orzo, uncooked
  • 4 1/2 cups chicken broth
  • 3 cups chopped kale
  • 3/4 cup grated Parmesan, plus more to serve

Method

  1. Heat 1 1/2 Tbsp oil in a large, deep lidded skillet (or Dutch oven) over medium-high. Brown the turkey with a little of the salt and pepper, ~6 minutes. Move to a plate.
  2. Add the remaining oil, onion, garlic, sweet potato, thyme, sage, poultry seasoning, smoked paprika, and the rest of the salt and pepper. Cook 4–5 minutes.
  3. Stir in the orzo to coat. Add the broth, bring to a boil, then cover and simmer on medium-low 10–12 minutes, stirring now and then, until the orzo is tender and most of the liquid is absorbed.
  4. Return the turkey, add the kale, cover, and cook 2–3 minutes until wilted.
  5. Off heat, stir in the Parmesan. Serve with more on top.

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.5 from Budget Bytes' serves-4. That's a lot for one skillet — a 5–6 qt Dutch oven is the safer pan.
  • No rice in this one (orzo is the grain), so the brown-rice rule doesn't apply.
  • Poultry seasoning is the same jar Thanksgiving uses — not a one-off.
  • Not Lemon Chicken Orzo: different protein, sweet potato + sage instead of lemon + spinach.
  • Mediterranean ✓ on 3 of 4: olive oil (swapped in for "cooking oil"), poultry, sweet potato + kale; orzo is refined. moderate for the pasta + Parmesan.
