Allergy: Contains fish or shellfish — not for a fish-allergic eater; plan a simple fish-free fallback

Bowl format (like the chicken & rice bowls the family already likes) with salmon swapped in — low-effort bridge to getting fish on the table.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb salmon fillets (frozen OK)
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil
  • 1 teaspoon garlic (minced or powder)
  • 3 cups cooked white rice
  • Cucumber, shredded carrot, or edamame for topping

Method

  1. Whisk teriyaki sauce (soy, honey, vinegar, sesame oil, garlic).
  2. Broil salmon 10–12 minutes until flaking, brushing with sauce halfway through.
  3. Serve over rice, top with vegetables and remaining sauce.

Notes

  • Each person builds their own bowl — good for picky eaters.
  • Second fish-night option to rotate with Honey Garlic Salmon.
  • Fish-free fallback: Bowl format is perfect — teriyaki-glazed chicken thigh instead of salmon, same sauce, same broil time. Cook alongside. The fish-free plate gets the same bowl experience.
  • 2026-07-19: ~4 stars family-wide BUT slightly overcooked in the convection oven — reduce temp ~25° or shorten broil; still dialing in time/temp.
