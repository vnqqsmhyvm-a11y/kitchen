Honey Garlic Glazed Beef

Spoon&Soul's honey garlic beef: cornstarch-coated sirloin cubes seared in batches, then tossed in a glossy honey-garlic-soy sauce with red chilies and green onion. Served over brown rice with a steamed vegetable. Not Beef and Broccoli (thin-sliced, broccoli cooked in, ginger-soy sauce) and not Korean Beef Bowls (ground beef bulgogi bowls).

Amounts below are scaled ×1.5 to serve 6; the source amount (serves 4) is in brackets.

Ingredients

Beef

  • 900 g (about 2 lb) beef steak such as sirloin, cut into bite-size cubes [600 g / about 1.3 lb]
  • 3 Tbsp cornstarch [2 Tbsp]
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp soy sauce [1 Tbsp]
  • 3/4 tsp black pepper [1/2 tsp]
  • 3/4 tsp paprika [1/2 tsp]
  • 3 Tbsp vegetable oil [2 Tbsp]

Honey garlic sauce

  • 6 Tbsp honey (1/4 cup + 2 Tbsp) [4 Tbsp]
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced [4]
  • 4 1/2 Tbsp soy sauce [3 Tbsp]
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp hoisin sauce, or vegetarian mushroom "oyster" sauce — house swap, see Notes [source: 1 Tbsp oyster sauce]
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp rice vinegar or lemon juice [1 Tbsp]
  • 3/4 tsp chili flakes — optional / adults, see Notes [1/2 tsp]
  • 6 Tbsp (90 ml) beef stock or water [1/4 cup / 60 ml]
  • 1 1/2 tsp cornstarch mixed with 1 1/2 Tbsp water [1 tsp + 1 Tbsp]
  • 1 1/2 tsp sesame oil [1 tsp]

Garnish

  • 3 red chilies, sliced — optional / on the side for kids [2]
  • 3 green onions, chopped [2]
  • 1 1/2 tsp sesame seeds [1 tsp]
  • Cilantro, optional

To serve

  • Brown rice, cooked (house rule: always brown)
  • Steamed broccoli, carrots, or bok choy

Method

  1. Pat the beef dry. Toss with the soy sauce, pepper and paprika; marinate 10–15 minutes, then toss in the cornstarch.
  2. Sear the beef in hot oil in batches, 2–4 minutes per side. Set aside.
  3. Over medium heat, cook the garlic 30–45 seconds. Add the honey, soy sauce, hoisin, vinegar, chili flakes and stock; simmer 2–3 minutes.
  4. Stir in the cornstarch slurry and cook 1–2 minutes until glossy. Stir in the sesame oil.
  5. Return the beef and toss with the chilies and half the green onions, 1–2 minutes. Thin with stock or reduce as needed.
  6. Garnish with sesame seeds and the remaining green onions. Serve over brown rice with the steamed vegetable.

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.5 from Spoon&Soul's 600 g beef / serves 4 (beef rounded to 900 g, about 2 lb). Source amounts in brackets.
  • House change — fish-free: the source uses 1 Tbsp oyster sauce. Oyster sauce is made from oysters (shellfish), and the deck treats that as off-limits for the fish-allergic eater — same swap as Chinese Cashew Chicken and Shrimp Fried Rice. This card uses hoisin (or a vegetarian mushroom "oyster" sauce) instead. Don't reintroduce oyster sauce without checking. No other fish/shellfish in the recipe (no fish sauce, no Worcestershire); check the hoisin label to be safe.
  • House change — rice: source just says rice; served with brown rice per the house rule.
  • House change — kid heat: fresh red chilies and chili flakes are optional — leave them out of the pan and put them on the side for adults, or add them to an adult portion at the end.
  • Source tips: use a tender cut, sear in small batches (crowding steams it), and keep the heat moderate once the honey goes in so it doesn't burn.
  • Sirloin: Sam's (proteins in quantity) or Aldi.
  • Red meat — counts toward the ≤2 red/processed cap.
  • Mediterranean ✗: red meat, vegetable oil, vegetables only as a side — only brown rice clears (1 of 4). moderate on the strength of the brown rice + steamed vegetable plate; honey glaze and red-meat-centric, so skipping the vegetable pushes it toward heavy.
