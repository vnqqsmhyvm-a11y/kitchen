Sesame Tofu with Broccoli

Budget Bytes' sesame tofu: pressed extra-firm tofu cubes dusted in cornstarch and pan-fried until crispy, then tossed with broccoli in a sweet sesame-ginger-garlic soy glaze over rice. Takeout-style and the gateway tofu dish for kids — it tastes like sesame chicken.

Ingredients

Sauce

  • 1/2 cup low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 2 Tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup rice vinegar
  • 2 Tbsp grated fresh ginger (or jar)
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/4 cup sesame seeds
  • 2 Tbsp cornstarch

Tofu and broccoli

  • 2 blocks (14 oz each) extra-firm tofu
  • 2 pinches salt
  • 4 Tbsp cornstarch
  • 4 Tbsp neutral oil, divided
  • 1 lb frozen broccoli florets
  • 6 green onions, sliced
  • 7–8 cups cooked brown rice

Method

  1. Press the tofu: wrap each block in towels, set a plate and a few cans on top, and press at least 30 minutes.
  2. Stir all sauce ingredients together until the sugar and cornstarch dissolve.
  3. Cut the tofu into 1-inch cubes, salt lightly, and toss with the cornstarch, 1 Tbsp at a time, until evenly coated.
  4. Heat 2 Tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium. Fry half the tofu, turning until golden and crisp on all sides; move to a plate. Repeat with the remaining oil and tofu.
  5. Add the frozen broccoli to the hot skillet and stir-fry until lightly browned at the edges. Lower heat to medium-low.
  6. Stir the sauce and pour it in; stir until it bubbles and thickens (fast). Turn off the heat and fold in the tofu.
  7. Serve over brown rice with green onions.

Notes

  • Scaled ×2 from Budget Bytes' 1 block / serves 3. Fry the tofu in two batches — crowding steams it.
  • Extra-firm tofu and rice vinegar are regular-grocery items (Aldi carries tofu; Sam's carries it in 2-packs some stores). The 45-minute "prep" in the original is mostly unattended pressing.
  • Fish-allergy safe: no fish or oyster sauce.
  • Not Copycat Panda Teriyaki Chicken or Beef and Broccoli — first tofu dinner in the deck.
  • Mediterranean ✓ by the house Mediterranean test: legume (soy) protein, broccoli, brown rice — 3 of 4 (neutral oil misses). moderate for the pan-frying and brown sugar.
