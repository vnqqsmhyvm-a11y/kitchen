Falafel Bowls

Oven falafel from canned chickpeas over brown rice with hummus, cucumber, tomato, feta. Vegetarian night that still feels like a bowl-bar. Kid-buildable.

Ingredients

Falafel

  • 3 cans (15 oz) chickpeas, drained, rinsed, and patted dry
  • 1/2 red onion, roughly chopped
  • 1 handful parsley (or cilantro, or both)
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • Pinch cayenne (skip for kids)
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ~1/3 cup flour (add until the mix holds)
  • Olive oil, for brushing

Bowls

  • Brown rice, ~4 cups cooked (house rule: always brown)
  • 1 tub hummus
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 4 oz crumbled feta
  • Optional: pita, greens, tzatziki, pickled onion (see Cava Night)

Method

  1. Heat oven to 400°F. Line a sheet pan with parchment.
  2. Pulse chickpeas, onion, herbs, garlic, salt, cumin, and cayenne in a food processor until a chunky paste — not hummus. Scrape into a bowl. Stir in baking powder. Add flour 2 Tbsp at a time until you can roll a ball that doesn't smear on your hands.
  3. Scoop 2 Tbsp portions, roll, flatten slightly. You want ~24 small patties. Brush tops with olive oil.
  4. Bake 20–25 minutes until the tops look dry and the bottoms are golden. No need to flip. Rest 5 minutes (they firm up).
  5. Build bowls: rice, 3–4 falafel, hummus, cucumber, tomato, feta.

Notes

  • Mix is Budget Bytes canned-chickpea falafel (they shallow-fry). Oven path is the weeknight version so we're not standing at a skillet for 6 people — brush with olive oil, 400°F, same mix. Scaled from 2 cans (~19 falafel) to 3 cans for a dinner protein.
  • Frozen falafel from Aldi is an OK skip-the-processor night if it's on the shelf. Don't hunt a brand at a third store.
  • No walnuts, no tahini, no chickpea flour — those are one-offs. All-purpose flour is already in the house.
  • Not Sheet-Pan Chickpeas, Feta & Tomatoes: that's roasted whole chickpeas + feta as the meal. These are formed falafel patties.
  • Mediterranean ✓: olive oil, legumes, vegetables, brown rice. light.
  • Full bar night: Cava Night.
