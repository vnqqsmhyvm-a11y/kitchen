Spinach Ricotta Chicken Bake

Chicken breasts baked under a ricotta-spinach layer and marinara, topped with mozzarella and parmesan. Cheese-heavy, so this is a heavy-tier night, not a Mediterranean one.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into 6 pieces
  • 1½ teaspoons salt, divided
  • ½ teaspoon pepper
  • 16 oz ricotta cheese
  • 8 oz frozen spinach, thawed and squeezed dry
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 egg (optional)
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese
  • ¼ cup parmesan cheese

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. Pour half the marinara into a large baking dish. Place chicken on top and season with ½ teaspoon each of salt and pepper.
  3. In a small bowl, combine ricotta cheese, spinach, garlic, and remaining teaspoon of salt. Optionally mix in the egg for added protein.
  4. Top chicken with the ricotta mixture, then pour remaining marinara sauce over top. Add cheeses now or 10 minutes before baking ends, depending on desired browning.
  5. Bake uncovered 30–40 minutes until chicken reaches 165°F internal temperature. Garnish with fresh herbs if desired and serve warm.

Notes

  • Squeeze the spinach thoroughly — excess moisture waters down the sauce.
  • Use a meat thermometer; don't overbake.
  • Chicken thighs, part-skim ricotta, or fresh sautéed spinach all work as substitutes.
  • Freezer-friendly: assemble unbaked and freeze up to 6 months. Refrigerate baked leftovers up to 3–4 days.
