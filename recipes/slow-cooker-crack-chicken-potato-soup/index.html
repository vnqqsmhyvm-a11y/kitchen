Slow Cooker Crack Chicken Potato Soup

Ingredients

As written in the source (×1). The source gives no yield; Kitchen estimates these amounts feed the household of 6.

Base

  • 1.5 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts (or thighs)
  • 1.5 lb Russet or Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled, ½-inch cubes
  • 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 packet (1 oz) dry ranch seasoning
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Creamy finish (added at the end)

  • 8 oz block cream cheese, softened and cubed
  • 1.5 cups sharp cheddar, freshly shredded
  • ½ cup heavy cream (or whole milk)

Toppings

  • 8 slices bacon, cooked crisp and crumbled
  • 3–4 green onions, sliced
  • Extra cheddar (amount not specified)

Method

  1. Put the raw chicken and cubed potatoes in the bottom of a 6-quart slow cooker. Pour in the broth; sprinkle the ranch, garlic powder, onion powder and pepper. Stir gently.
  2. Cover and cook on LOW 6–7 hours or HIGH 3–4 hours, until the potatoes are fork-tender and the chicken reaches 165°F.
  3. Remove the chicken, shred it, and return it. Add the cream cheese, cream and cheddar; cover and cook on LOW 15–20 minutes until melted.
  4. Stir until smooth; optionally mash some potatoes to thicken.
  5. Serve topped with bacon, green onions and extra cheddar.

Notes

  • Yield: not stated by the source. "Planned for 6" is a Kitchen estimate, not a source fact.
  • Cook-time discrepancy in the source: the method body says LOW 6–7 h / HIGH 3–4 h; the step heading says LOW 4–5 h. This card follows the body (LOW 6–7 h + 15–20 min finish). Doneness — potatoes fork-tender, chicken 165°F — is what controls.
  • Source variants only: breast or thigh; heavy cream or whole milk; Russet or Yukon Gold. No Instant Pot method given.
  • a fish-free plate / hidden fish: no fish. Check the ranch packet and broth labels for anchovy/fish/Worcestershire as usual.
