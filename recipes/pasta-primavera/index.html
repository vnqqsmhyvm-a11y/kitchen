Pasta Primavera

Vegetable pasta — broccoli, zucchini, peas, tomatoes — in a light olive-oil + splash-of-cream sauce. Kid-buildable: picky eaters can fish out the pasta and the veg they like.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb short pasta (penne or rotini)
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp unsalted butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 small yellow onion, diced
  • 3 cups broccoli florets (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 medium zucchini, halved and sliced
  • 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved (or 3 Roma, diced)
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 1/2 cup chicken or vegetable broth
  • 1/3 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan, plus more to serve
  • Juice of 1/2 lemon
  • Salt and pepper
  • Optional: leftover cooked chicken, sliced, stirred in at the end

Method

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water until al dente. Drain, saving 1/2 cup pasta water.
  2. Meanwhile, heat olive oil and butter in a large skillet over medium. Add onion; cook 3–4 minutes until soft. Add garlic 30 seconds.
  3. Add broccoli and zucchini, salt, and pepper. Cook 5–6 minutes until the broccoli is tender-crisp (frozen broccoli just needs to heat through). Add tomatoes; cook 2 minutes until they start to collapse.
  4. Pour in broth and cream. Bring to a gentle simmer. Stir in peas, Parmesan, and lemon juice.
  5. Toss in the pasta. Loosen with pasta water if it looks tight. Optional chicken now. Pass more Parmesan.

Notes

  • Simply Recipes classic uses butter, cream, a blanch-and-shock for broccoli/asparagus/snow peas, and 1/2 lb pasta. House version: 1 lb pasta for 6, olive oil as the primary fat with 1 Tbsp butter, cream cut to 1/3 cup, Aldi-staple veg (broccoli, zucchini, peas, tomatoes) instead of asparagus and snow peas.
  • Not a cream-bomb Alfredo — vegetables are the point. moderate because of the splash of cream + pasta, not heavy.
  • Mediterranean ✓: olive oil, vegetables actually in the dish, vegetarian. Regular pasta misses whole-ish carbs — 3 of 4.
  • Leftovers: the veg softens. Fine for lunch; not a great make-ahead.
