Greek Turkey Burgers

Budget Bytes' Mediterranean turkey burgers — ground turkey mixed with spinach, sun-dried tomato, red onion, feta, and oregano, on buns with a lemon-dill yogurt sauce and cucumber. Burger night that counts as a Mediterranean night.

Ingredients

Lemon-dill yogurt sauce

  • 2/3 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 tsp lemon juice
  • 1/8 tsp garlic powder (generous pinch)
  • 1/4 tsp dried dill
  • Pinch salt

Burgers

  • 1.5 lb ground turkey (93% lean)
  • 1/3 cup finely chopped sun-dried tomatoes
  • 1/3 cup minced red onion, plus thin slices to serve
  • 2 1/2 oz frozen spinach (about 1 1/4 cups frozen), thawed and squeezed very dry
  • 1/3 cup crumbled feta
  • 1 1/4 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/3 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/3 tsp salt
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 6 hamburger buns (whole wheat if you have them)
  • 1 cucumber, sliced

Method

  1. Stir together the yogurt, lemon juice, garlic powder, dill, and salt. Refrigerate.
  2. Chop the sun-dried tomatoes, mince the onion, crumble the feta, and squeeze the spinach dry (it should shrink to ~1/3 cup).
  3. Mix the turkey, sun-dried tomatoes, onion, feta, spinach, oregano, garlic powder, and salt until just combined.
  4. Shape into 6 patties — flatter and wider than you think, they shrink.
  5. Heat half the oil in a large skillet over medium. Cook 3 patties 5–7 minutes per side until browned and 165°F. Repeat with the rest.
  6. Bun + yogurt sauce + burger + cucumber + red onion.

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.25 from Budget Bytes' 19 oz turkey / 5 burgers to 1.5 lb / 6 burgers.
  • Sun-dried tomatoes: buy the dry-pack bag or a jar — and use the rest in Greek Turkey & Rice Skillet so it isn't a one-off.
  • Grill option: chill the patties 30 minutes so they hold, then grill over medium on an oiled grate.
  • Kid build: plain bun + ketchup works; the spinach is hidden.
  • Mediterranean ✓ on 3 of 4: olive oil, poultry, vegetables in and on the burger; buns are the refined part. light.
