Beef Kofta with Roasted Vegetables

Budget Bytes' Middle Eastern kofta meatballs — beef with red onion, garlic, parsley, cumin, oregano, and a whisper of cinnamon and cloves — over brown rice with a sheet pan of roasted zucchini, squash, onion, and grape tomatoes. Warm spice, not hot.

Ingredients

Roasted vegetables

  • 2 zucchini, diced
  • 2 yellow squash, diced
  • 1 1/2 pints grape tomatoes
  • 1 red onion (most of it — save 1/3 for the meatballs), 1-inch pieces
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1/3 tsp garlic powder
  • 3/4 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper

Kofta

  • 1.5 lb ground beef
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/3 red onion, minced
  • 3 Tbsp chopped parsley, plus more to garnish
  • 3/4 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/3 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon (scant)
  • 1/4 tsp ground cloves (scant)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil (for the skillet)

To serve: brown rice, about 6 cups cooked (house rule: always brown); optional plain yogurt or tzatziki

Method

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss the zucchini, squash, onion pieces, and tomatoes with olive oil, garlic powder, oregano, salt, and pepper on a large sheet pan (two if crowded). Roast 40 minutes, stirring halfway.
  2. Start the brown rice.
  3. Mix the beef, minced onion, garlic, parsley, oregano, cumin, cinnamon, cloves, and salt. Shape into ~24 ping-pong-ball meatballs (or mini patties — they're delicate).
  4. Heat 1 1/2 Tbsp oil in a large nonstick or cast-iron skillet over medium. Brown half the meatballs on all sides, ~7 minutes. Repeat.
  5. Plate rice, vegetables, and four kofta each; parsley on top, yogurt on the side.

Notes

  • Scaled ×1.5 from Budget Bytes' 1 lb beef / serves 4.
  • Swapped the skillet "cooking oil" for olive oil.
  • Cinnamon and cloves are the same pantry jars Chicken Shawarma Bowls uses.
  • Not Spaghetti & Meatballs or Tzatziki Bowls: spiced Middle Eastern meatballs, no pasta, no taco seasoning.
  • Red meat — counts toward the ≤2 cap. Mediterranean ✓ on 3 of 4 (olive oil, a full sheet pan of vegetables, brown rice); beef is the miss. moderate.
